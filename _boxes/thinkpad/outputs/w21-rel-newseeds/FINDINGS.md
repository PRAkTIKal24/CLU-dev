## What would have proved the extension wrong or worthless
- Integrity: a config hash other than 0d4ec5b9… in any run JSON, or replay hashes or numbers that do
  not reproduce. Checked: all six REL JSONs carry the hash (the tables generator asserts it), all
  replay hashes match and all numbers reproduce.
- Store vs coding: a `+rel` lift of about 0 on the new seeds would have put the margin in the store.
  Instead the lift is +0.199/+0.277/+0.000 and CLU − best `+rel` is ≤ 0.006, so on the new seeds the
  margin is the coding.
- Controls: a control reaching the CLU makes the store not load-bearing on that seed. That happens on
  seed 4, on TEST and on VAL.
- Matched rivals: a matched rival reaching the CLU removes the margin. That happens on seed 5
  (transformer @80k, 0.939 = 0.939).
