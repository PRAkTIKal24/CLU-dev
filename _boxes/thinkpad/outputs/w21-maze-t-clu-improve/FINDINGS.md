## 0. The answer

**Yes. Changing only the CLU widens its CMAZE-T composition margin under identical inputs, and
the scored TEST runs read WIN by the Head's rule. Most of the gain comes from ONE design change:
colour-relational coding of the request.**

| | CLU | best matched rival R\* (both budgets) | per seed CLU / best matched | label |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **w19 A1, BOTH TEST** (quoted from w19) | 0.725 | 0.626 (lstm 80k) | 0.800/0.627 · 0.566/0.567 · 0.808/0.784 | LOSS under w19's +0.10 bar |
| **w21 CLU-REL-A10, BOTH VAL** (free) | **0.937** | 0.685 (transformer 80k) | 0.903/0.811 · 0.914/0.569 · 0.994/0.675 | **WIN** |
| **w21 CLU-REL-A10, BOTH TEST (runs 7–9)** | **0.929** | 0.685 (transformer 80k) | 0.908/0.803 · 0.894/0.567 · 0.984/0.685 | **WIN** |

Sources: `test/LABEL-BOTH-test.json`, `val_full/LABEL-BOTH-val.json`, w19
`test/BARS-BOTH-test.json`.

**The mechanism: the COLOUR-RELATIONAL CODING of the request.** A1's held-out n-way failures
are all-or-nothing per held-out tuple (§2):
- The RULE-B tuples (pocket = c, tint = c, with no door or an open door) fail because, for that
  colour c, the (pocket = c, tint = c) combination never occurs in train.
- A design built from colour IDENTITY (one-hots, pairs, triples) therefore has nothing to read,
  and neither does a network given the same one-hot/pair input. Every matched rival on the frozen
  input sits at exactly **0.5674** on TEST seed 1 (0.5689 on VAL seed 1) for this reason.
- The new CLU adds `eq(x, y)` indicators: x and y are colour words and name the SAME colour. It
  also crosses them with the door and direction one-hots. "pocket equals tint" is then one
  column, learnable from other colours.
- Those rows go from 0 to 1.000. The (c, c, c) INTERACTION family is **still 0 on every seed**.

⚠ **Read the WIN with its printed diagnostic.** The same relational columns, appended to every
rival's frozen input (the `+rel` diagnostic: matched floats, frozen recipe, never a bar), lift
the best rival from 0.685 to **0.862** on TEST (transformer + rel, 80k: 0.908 / 0.869 / 0.809).
So about 0.18 of the 0.24 margin is the coding, which any learner can be given. The CLU keeps
**+0.066** on the mean over the best `+rel` rival. Against the best `+rel` arm per seed
(0.915 / 0.869 / 0.819) it is ahead on 2 of 3 seeds: behind mlp + rel 80k on seed 0 (0.908 vs
0.915).

The claim therefore is:
- **(i)** The CLU, with a relational request design, beats every matched rival on the frozen
  input on every seed (the Head's WIN), on BOTH TEST and on BOTH / WORDS-FULL VAL.
- **(ii)** The part of that margin attributable to the CLU's store/closed-form read, rather than
  to the coding, is small (+0.07 mean on BOTH TEST). It is not ahead on every seed, and it is
  **zero on WORDS-FULL VAL**: a matched transformer given the same relational columns reads
  0.941 vs CLU 0.937 (§5).
- **Mechanism, named: colour-relational request coding (an input representation), carried
  through the CLU's closed-form head.** It is not a store effect. The store gate holds on means
  but is weaker than A1's (§3 ⚠).

**Budget: runs 7, 8, 9 of 9 SPENT** (BOTH TEST, seeds 0/1/2). Each had a ledger row appended
before launch (`/c/chlu/SCORED-LEDGER.md` 2026-09-24T05:20:26Z / 05:29:07Z / 05:37:46Z, plus
the outcome row). Runs 4–6 belong to spoke/words-full-test and were not touched.

## 1. What was changed (ONLY the CLU) and what was frozen

**Frozen and unchanged:**
- banks (VAL base+200k, TEST base+300k), hold-outs, the `exact` judge, the w9 state encoder, the
  codec (latent 128, 2,500 steps);
- rival architectures and recipes (8,000 / 80,000 steps) and the read-path-float matching rule;
- the per-condition input: BOTH = observation ⊕ the 318 design for every rival, with the CLU
  reading the true words; WORDS-FULL = the 318 design for every arm;
- the learned blind arms (A1 design; they are falsifiers, not the candidate), the floors, and
  the control recipes;
- across-wells is a **DERANGEMENT, asserted and printed**: `[1, 0]` on every seed of every run
  (`across_wells_derangement` in every JSON).

**Rivals:** re-matched automatically to the new CLU's 9,200 read floats by the standing rule.
- mlp 9,698 (+5.4 %), lstm 9,165 (−0.4 %), transformer 9,269 (+0.8 %), on a 954-dim input.
- For comparison, w19 matched them at ≈ 4,600 floats. At 9,200 the rivals get twice A1's
  capacity; the harder comparison was accepted rather than avoided (TABLES §4).

**The frozen CLU — `prereg/CLU-REL-A10.json`** (sha256 `0d4ec5b9…`), code `scripts/w21_clu.py`:
- K 2, **10 atoms**, ridge 1.0, latent 128.
- Design width 396 = `[bias | main 27 | 0.5 × pair 290 | eq 6 | eq × door 42 | eq × dir 30]`.
  - `eq` = the 6 pairs of colour words (pocket, key, door-closed-colour, tint).
  - pair × 0.5 is exactly a ridge of 4 on the pair block, i.e. hierarchical shrinkage.
- Same `CovContentStore`, same Ward partition, same closed-form `solve_head`, same `head_read`
  with no feature argument, same exact downdate.
- Read floats = 1,280 atoms + 7,920 address book = **9,200**: 36,800 B deployed (f32),
  convention = 4 × (atom + address-book floats). The float64 statistic is printed beside it in
  TABLES §4 and never netted.
- ⚠ In every w21 runner JSON, `config` is the runner's A1 cfg, and only the frozen blind arms
  use it. The CLU, its 4 controls and the deletion objects are built from `w21_clu_cfg`; see
  `clu_ledger` (read_floats 9,200, design_width 396).

## 2. The VAL search — 68 candidates (+ the A1 reproduction), every one logged (LOG.md; TABLES §0; `val/sweep_r*.json`)

**Method:** closed-form CLU fits on a cache of the frozen VAL instrument (`scripts/w21_cache.py`:
the w19 path exactly). **Instrument check:** A1 on the cache = **0.8019 / 0.5689 / 0.8034**, equal
to w19's VAL A1 to every printed digit.

**Gate:** mean > 0.725 AND no seed below A1.

| round | what | result |
|---|---|---|
| r1 (Advisor's 1–3) | pair shrinkage ×0.5/×0.25/×2, ridge 0.1/10, all triples, pocket×door×tint triple, the 31 dynamics scalars as columns | **all FAIL**: n-way exactly 0.8019 / 0.5689 / 0.8034 for every one (all triples: 0.4785 on seed 1). Shrinkage DOES help unseen 2-way (pair 0.406 → 0.439, Advisor's point 2 confirmed on `pair`), but n-way does not move |
| diagnostic | per held-out tuple (`logs/diag_nway.log`) | failures = RULE-B tuples with no same-colour training support + the (c, c, c) interaction; everything decided by seen colour identity = 1.000 |
| r2 | colour-relational block (`eq`, `eq3`), crossed with nothing / dir / door+dir / all | uncrossed = A1 exactly (FAIL); ×dir 0.7155 (FAIL, seed 2 drops); ×door+dir **0.8755 PASS**; ×all 0.8451–0.8842 PASS |
| r3 | 36-cell grid {eq, eq3} × {door+dir, pocket+door+tint+dir, all} × rel_scale {1, 3, 10} × pair_scale {1, 0.5} | all 36 PASS, 0.7990–0.9135; leader eq × (door, dir), pair 0.5 = 0.9135 (0.9029 / 0.9141 / 0.9236), 5,520 floats, flat in rel_scale |
| r4 (Advisor's 3) | pair 0.25 / 0.1, ridge 0.3 / 3, atoms 4 / 8 / 12, K 1 / 3 / 4, + pocket cross, main ×2 | atoms 12 = **0.9371** (0.9029 / 0.9141 / 0.9943); K 1 = 0.9371 but **inadmissible** (one well cannot be deranged, so the frozen across-wells control cannot exist); others 0.8781–0.9135 |
| r5 | atoms 9 / 10 / 16, atoms 12 + eq3, atoms 12 + pair 1 | atoms 10 / 12 / 16 and eq3 tie at 0.9371; atoms 9 0.9258; pair 1 0.9035 |

**Selection** (rule D3, declared before r3): maximum mean, with ties broken by fewer floats →
**atoms 10**. Gate: 0.9029 ≥ 0.8019, 0.9141 ≥ 0.5689, 0.9943 ≥ 0.8034, mean 0.9371 > 0.725 →
**KEPT**. Frozen, hashed, PREREG'd.

**Residual on VAL** (`logs/diag_nway_frozen.log`):
- Every RULE-B tuple A1 missed is now 1.000 (seed 2's (3, 0, 3): 0.353 → 0.660).
- The (c, c, c) interaction family stays at **0.000 on every seed**. `eq3`, the 3-way
  same-colour indicator, did not fix it: those tuples have no training support in most seeds.

## 4. Deletion — on every scored run (TABLES §6)

- **Store-scoped exactness:** relative ΔS 7.7e-16 … 1.2e-15 (bar 1e-5), deployed-atom max |Δ|
  0.0, all 6 (seed × mode) pass.

  for both modes (value: 8,598 / 8,185 / 6,937 rows deleted; colour ∧ tinted-floor: 3,361 /
  2,169 / 2,238).

  other design column.
- The rival deletes only by retrain-without: 112–169 s and 8,000 gradient steps.
- Frozen codec and state encoder are declared OUTSIDE the deletable object.

## 7. What would have proved this wrong, and what happened
- **No candidate clearing the VAL gate** → a declared negative with 0 runs. **Did not happen:**
  57 of 68 candidates cleared it (⚠ PREREG §1 and the ledger rows say "69"; that count wrongly included the A1 reproduction. Corrected here, not in the hashed PREREG or the append-only ledger). The Advisor's first two ranked ideas (dynamics scalars, pair
  shrinkage) did NOT: n-way unchanged to every digit.
- **CLU not ahead of R\* on the TEST mean / on a majority of seeds.** Did not happen: ahead
  3/3.
- **Controls not collapsing.** On the mean they collapse enough (0.601 ≤ 0.779). Seed 0's
  across-wells does not, and the store is measurably less binding than A1's (§3 ⚠).
- **`+rel` rivals matching the CLU.** Partially happened: they close ~75 % of the gap (0.685 →
  0.862 vs CLU 0.929) and beat the CLU on seed 0 (mlp + rel 80k 0.915 vs 0.908). **The margin is
  mostly the relational coding, an input representation, not the store.** On WORDS-FULL VAL
  they fully match it (transformer + rel 0.941 vs CLU 0.937). Named as such.
