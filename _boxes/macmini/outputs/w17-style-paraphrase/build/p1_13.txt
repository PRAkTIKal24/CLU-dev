 009 ABSTRACT
 010
 011 Aworldmodeltrusted with requests it has never seen needs three things at once:
 012 its outputs are valid under an exact judge, it composes held-out combinations of
 013 factors from factors it has seen, and it can forget specific training rows exactly.
 014 Compositional generation is extensively studied and exact unlearning established;
 015 their intersection, however, is empty: a mechanical screen of approximately 4,050
 016 works finds no generative model that deletes training rows exactly. We therefore
 017 study the simplest object with all three properties, the Naive CLU: a label-free
 018 store of per-well principal subspaces, computed on the latents of a frozen encoder,
 019 read through a request-indexed address book and rendered by a frozen decoder; its
 content is derived from a retained sufficient statistic. Because deletion downdates
 020 the statistic rather than the truncated factors, the store after deletion equals the
 021 store fitted without the deleted rows. On a Shapes3D value hold-out, with the par-
 022 tition selected on validation (K⋆ = 32 wells), the store composes held-out pairs
 023 at 32.350 ± 0.815 and held-out six-factor corners at 67.177 ± 1.320 distinct valid
 024 novel tuples per corner over three seeds. It beats every read-path-matched learned
 025 rival on 109 of 109 corners on every seed, and all four landscape controls collapse
 026 to exactly 0.0000. Deletion is exact and store-scoped, but it is not free: across the
 027 three seeds, the object that forgets is 4.0768 to 4.0897 times the 524,288-byte ref-
 028 erence in its four-part ledger. We show that on this object deletion changes what
 029 the store holds but not what it answers. On a colour maze with an exact integer
 −16 −16
 030 judge, deletion transfers (relative residual 7.522 × 10 to 8.826 × 10 , pro-
 jector residual 0.000), whereas composition does not transfer through the store:
 031 moving the composition verb from latent space to plan space lifts held-out va-
 032 lidity by +0.4422 to +0.5354, but it lifts a matched-bytes lookup floor further,
 033 which places the composition operator in the interface. We conclude with what
 034 the naive unit cannot do and with the one measured clue toward a dynamical suc-
 035 cessor: the store is exactly the zero-dynamics limit of a settling unit, its zero-step
 036 armbit-identical to the static read.

 038 1 INTRODUCTION: POSITIONING, AND WHY THE INTERSECTION MATTERS
 039
 040 1.1 POSITIONING: COMPOSITIONAL GENERATION IS CROWDED; WITH EXACT DELETION IT
 041 IS EMPTY
 042
 043 Generating held-out combinations of factors is a long-standing and active problem. The multilin-
 044 ear line (Tenenbaum and Freeman, 2000; TensorFaces, Vasilescu and Terzopoulos, 2002; Vlasic et
 045 al., 2005; MLC-VAE, 2020) factorises a data tensor indexed by every label combination and ren-
 046 ders linearly. The frozen-generator line (GANSpace, 2020; TCA, 2021; PandA, 2023, and its 2024
 047 journal version) identifies editable directions in a pretrained generator, without labels and without
 048 an address book. We claim neither half. Specifically, we do not claim multilinear or bilinear fac-
 049 tored generation, a request-indexed tensor head, tensor-product label conditioning, or the downdate
 050 primitive; the latter follows in one line from the summation form of Cao and Yang (2015), since a
 051 covariance is a summation.
 052 Exact unlearning is likewise established: for k-means (Ginart et al., 2019), for bilinear matrix com-
 053 pletion, and for ridge heads on frozen backbones, where the most recent work explicitly restricts
 itself to ridge regression and excludes principal-component, eigendecomposition and generative
 1
 054 models. The closest generative attempts are approximate. Feature unlearning for pretrained GANs
 055 and VAEs unlearns a feature rather than rows and fine-tunes the generator; data redaction from pre-
 056 trained GANs is stated to be a task different from deletion; and GAN unlearning by substitution is
 057 approximate by construction.
 058 However, on a systematic screen the intersection of the two literatures is empty. A forward-citation
 059 crawl over every title and abstract citing the canonical works of either side screened approximately
 060 4,050 unique works: 3,038 citing the generation-side anchors, 759 citing the deletion-side anchors,
 061 254 from 21 orthogonal queries that cite neither, and about 100 from a final targeted sweep. Of the
 062 3,038 generation-side works, deletion language appears in 10 (0.3%), seven of them false positives
 063 and none exact; no work among the 3,038 combines frozen-decoder, tensor-head and label-index
 064 language (0 of 3,038); no single work reaches more than 4 of the 6 claim atoms; and none of
 065 the 759 deletion-side works combines principal-subspace, generative and frozen-model language.
 066 Theorthogonal query that poses the question directly, compositional ∧ generative ∧ exact deletion,
 067 returns zero works. We therefore state the position only in the form that the screen supports: the
 068 intersection of compositional generation with exact deletion is empty in the works we could screen.
 069 The residual risk is real, and §5 states it: abstract-level screening cannot see a match buried in a
 070 method section, and the indexing of preprints younger than about three months is incomplete.
 071 One distinction within this intersection is ours and merits a sentence. The decremental-PCA line
 072 (Hall, Marshall and Martin, 2002, and successors) subtracts deflated eigenspaces; its authors de-
 073 scribe the result as an approximation to the refit, report that the subtraction tends to instability, and
 074 give no rigorous error analysis. Our route is exact for the opposite reason: downdate the statistic,
 075 not the truncated factors. We downdate the full, untruncated second-moment statistic by subtraction
 076 and re-eigendecompose the content afterwards, so truncation error never enters the subtraction.

 078 1.2 WHYTHEINTERSECTION MATTERS: WORLD MODELS NEED ALL THREE AT ONCE
 079
 080 Aworldmodelisusefultothe extent that it can be queried for states and transitions it has not seen,
 081 and its answers trusted. We name three properties and use them throughout:
 082 • Rulevalidity: an output is valid if an exact judge, integer arithmetic with no tolerance, accepts it;
 083 approximate similarity to a training item does not count.
 084 • Composition: the model produces valid outputs for held-out combinations of factor values that it
 085 has seen only separately.
 086 • Exactdeletion: after a set of training rows is deleted, the deletable object equals, to floating-point
 087 roundoff, the object fitted without those rows.
 088 The three properties, however, pull against each other: a lookup table deletes exactly but composes
 089 nothingithasnotstored;alearnedgeneratorcomposesbutcandeleteonlybyretraining;andapprox-
 090 imate unlearning misses the exact answer in either direction (§3.4). The object we study therefore
 091 makes the three properties properties of one store, and we measure, row by row, where each holds
 092 and where it does not.

 122 2 THE OBJECT, THE JUDGE AND THE LEDGER
 123
 124 Aclaimaboutanyofthethreeproperties is only as precise as the definitions of the object, its judge
 125 and its byte ledger; we therefore fix these here, before any result.
 126
 127 The store. Afrozen encoder maps each training item to a latent. The latent space is partitioned
 128 without labels into K wells by a balanced correlation partition, and in each well the store keeps the
 129 leading principal subspace of the fit pool, with the pool mean as the first atom. No label enters the
 130 content. We write n for the number of factors a request composes jointly and K for the number of
 131 wells, and never write either as k.
 132 The read. Arequest is a tuple of factor values. A tensor-product address book, fitted on the fit
 133 pool, maps the request to per-well coefficients; the read renders x = U c in each well’s or-
 134 k k k
 thonormal basis and the frozen decoder renders the image. The read is request-only: no feature
 135 tensor enters it, as asserted on the deployed object (bitwise stable under feature perturbation over
 136 64 checks at K⋆). Labels thus touch the model only at the address book: this is the interface split
 137 of unsupervised content, a request-indexed head and a frozen renderer, with no Cartesian-product
 138 training requirement.
 139
 140 Theretainedstatisticandexactdeletion. Besidethecontent,thestorekeepsperwelltherunning
 141 covariance, mean and count of the fit rows. A deletion proceeds in three steps:
 142 • the contribution of the deleted rows is subtracted from the statistic;
 143 • the atoms are re-eigendecomposed from the downdated statistic;
 144 • the address book is carried across the basis change by a closed-form rotation formed at delete
 145 time.
 146 Norowofdataisread. Weuseonedefinition of exactness throughout, scoped before any table:

 148 EXACT IS STORE-SCOPED. After deleting a set of rows, the store content equals, to float
 149 roundoff, the content fitted from the retained rows alone, holding the frozen encoder and
 150 decoder fixed. Neither is refit at any point. It is not retrain-equivalence of the generative
 151 modelanditis not a claim that the renderer forgets.
 152 Wedeclare the frozen encoder and decoder outside the deletable object. The choice is forced: the
 153 frozen autoencoder’s own training set contains evaluation-bank items (543 / 529 / 552 on the three
 154 mazeseeds),soweightsfittedwiththedeletedrowspresentareuntouchedbyanydeletion. Wecarry
 155 the scopingsentenceourdeletiontheorystatesforitsownstore: theguaranteeisstore-levelonly; the
 156 frozenencoderandanyresidueofpastwritesinalearnedlandscapeareseparatechannels,measured
 157 separately; and we do not claim certified (ε,δ) unlearning. We keep two senses of “exact” apart:
 158 an exact judge is integer arithmetic with no tolerance; exact deletion is the store-scoped definition
 159 above.

 161 The four-part ledger, never netted. Every store row prints four byte counts separately: (1) the
 atoms, (2) the covariance and count statistic, (3) the address book, and (4) the coupling and hole
 3
 162 table used for refusal. We never net them into a single number, and every byte figure names its
 163 convention (symmetric-triangle float64 for the statistic, unless marked otherwise). The 524,288-
 164 byte figure recurring below is a reference column: the store of the earlier composition-only design
 165 was pinned there, and read-path-matched rivals carry about 131,072 float32 parameters at it. It is
 166 not a budget that the deletable object meets.
 167
 168 Rivals: naive against naive. Learned rivals (MLP, LSTM, transformer, and a continuous-input
 169 MLP) are fitted from the same frozen features and matched to the read path of the store. At K⋆
 170 the fitted rivals carry 130,433 to 131,891 parameters. The ledger is memory, not a read-path pa-
 171 rameter: it is printed, never matched. A lookup table of written items is a printed floor, never
 172 a rival. Before any row is claimed for the store, the charter controls (landscape removed,
 173 landscape shuffled, landscape shuffled across wells, random atoms) must
 174 collapse; if they do not, the row is an interface row or nothing.

 177 corner, with ceilings of 36 (pair corners) and 72 (six-factor corners). The estimand is paired over
 178 corners, with a margin of 1.0 tuple per corner declared before any number. Seed count is a column

 342 3.4 WHATTHERIVALSCANDOINSTEAD: RETRAIN WITHOUT, OR APPROXIMATE
 343
 344 Theonlyexactdeletion route for a learned rival is to retrain from scratch without the rows. We built

 507 4.3 COMPOSITION ON ROUTES: THE VERB IS THE LEVER, AND IT BELONGS TO THE
 508 INTERFACE
 509 The store’s read is linear in the request design, so composing two half-addresses averages their
 510 latents. An exact-judge route task, however, composes by union: the two decoded half-plans are
 511 overlaid, with the target’s plan winning conflicts. We therefore hold everything fixed but the verb:
 512 thesamestoreandcontrols,thesamerequest-onlyread,thesamefrozenencoderandshareddecoder,
 513 the same judge and split, and rivals at matched parameters, on CMAZE-2b, TEST, three scored
 514 seeds. AVG decodes the average of the two half-latents (the earlier read on the identical two latents),
 515 whereas MERGE decodes each half and overlays the plans. Nothing in the verb is fitted, and the
 516 address book is never refit for the halves.
 517
 518 The verb claim. Moving the composition of two half-addresses from latent space to plan space,
 519 on identical latents and through the identical frozen decoder, lifts the store’s held-out unseen-pair
 520 validity by +0.4422 / +0.5354 / +0.4471 on three of three seeds; it also lifts the n-way conjunction
 521 row from 0.0000 / 0.0160 / 0.0000 to 0.4891 / 0.4652 / 0.3915. The merge rule is load-bearing: on
 522 the identical two decoded plans, letting the agent’s plan win reads 0.2714 / 0.4848 / 0.4423, the agent
 523 half alone 0.1960 / 0.2424 / 0.2404, and the target half alone 0.0704 / 0.1010 / 0.1202. The geometry
 explains why averaging cannot work. The two half-latents are nearly parallel, cos(Z ,Z ) =
 524 A B
 525 0.9668 / 0.9589 / 0.9630, and their average is aligned with the full read at 0.9971 / 0.9964 / 0.9966;
 526 averaging thus returns a slightly shrunk copy of what the linear read already gives. The latent space
 527 has no room for a union, whereas the same two halves, decoded, are complementary in plan space.
 528 Whyit is an interface claim and not a store claim. The same fixed merge lifts every arm that
 529 reads anything. It lifts the matched-bytes lookup floor by +0.8299, against the store’s +0.4749, car-
 530 rying that floor to 0.9648 / 0.9646 / 0.9808 on unseen pairs, past the store and past the single-decode
 531 pipeline ceiling. The charter controls do not collapse: on the seen row, landscape shuffled
 532 reads 0.5111 / 0.5552 / 0.5727 and random atoms 0.5395 / 0.5650 / 0.5858, against a collapse
 533 bar of half the store’s score, 0.3606 / 0.3455 / 0.3508. Consequently, no row is claimed for the store
 534 under either verb. The verb claim survives because MERGE and AVG consume the same two latents:
 535 a verb claim is not a store claim, and only the verb claim is made. The composition operator is part
 536 of the interface, and it is task-shaped: images compose by mixing latents, whereas routes compose

 620 5.3 THE THEORY WE CARRY: THE LATENT DYNAMICS MODEL CLASS
 621 Wecall the class of objects of which this store is the static corner a Latent Dynamics Model: a
 622 store whose content is read by relaxing a latent state under a designed energy. The Naive CLU
 623 is the member of this class with the relaxation switched off. (The phrase is used in reduced-order
 624 modelling and in model-based reinforcement learning for a learned surrogate of an environment’s
 625 dynamics; here the dynamics are the read, not a model of the data.) Its laws are this paper’s own
 626 theory, each with a status label and rider (further laws in Appendix Q).
 627
 628 Twolawsmeasuredonthisstudy’sownstore(CONFIRMED [THIS STUDY]). Switchedon,the
 629 field store’s relaxation obeys a curvature–retention relationship whose prediction is a time, not a
 630 score. With nothing fitted, it predicts the settle half-life to a median factor of 1.105 on 49 held-out
 631 corners and three seeds (bar 0.10 on the log error; 0.0433 / 0.0435 / 0.0431), and beats a one-
 632 parameter form fitted with sight of the held-out measurements on 49 of 49 corners on every seed.
 633 One of six pre-registered bars (the V-curve argmin) misses on all three seeds, and the law predicts
 634 where its simplest form stops. Deletion commutes with scheduled decay on a learned store, 0 bytes
 635 differing in a 2,054,866-byte image. The stronger claim, that the post-delete store equals the never-
 636 inserted one, is REFUTED (65.17% to 65.39% of the image differs, 83.5% of it because the address
 637 book and coupling table are fitted on the pool). These are dynamics results about our own store.
 They occupy no row of the evaluation, they beat no model, and we make no capability claim from
 638 them; no arm of ours fails the retention law, so it distinguishes nothing. Full statements, bars and
 639 misses: Appendix P.

 641 5.4 LEAVING NOTE
 642
 643 The naive unit buys exact deletion by subtraction and, at a stated partition, composition that beats
 644 read-path-matched learners on Shapes3D. However, it does not buy composition on an exact-judge
 645 route task, where the operator is task-shaped and belongs to the interface; it does not stop the object
 646 answering a deleted value; and it pays for exactness in bytes. Switched on, the dynamics cost
 647 composition at every strength that moves the state, while a law that no arm of ours fails predicts
 their relaxation: so far, a law about the store, not a lever on the task. The experiment that would

 648 make the law a claim about a class, a comparator predicted in advance to be mispredicted in the
