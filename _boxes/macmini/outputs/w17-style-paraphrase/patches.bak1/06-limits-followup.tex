The store we evaluate is the zero-dynamics limit of the CLU: the same content, wells and read,
with the step count of the settling loop set to zero. Inside a well this is an algebraic
identity. The store's energy is the distance to the atom span, and the request's basin sits at

to go (derivation in Appendix~\ref{app:clue}). We asserted this in code: the zero-step arm was required to be bit-identical to
