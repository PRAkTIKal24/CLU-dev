\figslot{fig_maze_conditions}{\textbf{CMAZE-T: the n-CLU against the best matched baseline under
four input choices, and six seeds without and with relational coding.} (a)~Unseen $n$-way validity
under the engine's judge, one row per input choice: the full word design (test, seeds 0--2), the
observation (validation, seeds 0--2), and words plus observation for the n-CLU and for the n-CLU-REL
(test, seeds 0--5). Every seed of a row is drawn: circles are the n-CLU (n-CLU-REL in the last row),
diamonds the row's baseline, and a vertical tick marks the mean over the row's seeds. The baseline of
the first three rows is the matched baseline with the highest mean over the row's seeds, taken over
every family and both step limits; it is a Transformer in each row, at its declared training steps
for the full word design and at $10\times$ training steps otherwise. The baseline of the n-CLU-REL
row is Transformer-REL, the Transformer given the same relational columns, at $10\times$ training
steps (hollow diamonds). (b)~Words plus observation, test, seeds 0--5, without relational coding: per
seed, the n-CLU, the Transformer at $10\times$ training steps of the third row, and the highest of the
four controls on that seed. (c)~The same with relational coding: the n-CLU-REL, the Transformer-REL of
the last row, and the highest control. Every arm's per-seed value is in
Tab.~\ref{tab:cmazet-sym}.}{fig:maze-wm}

\figslot{fig_cmazet_families}{\textbf{Held-out CMAZE-T transitions, one per rule family, with every
model's next state (words plus observation, test).} Columns: $s_t$ with the action, the engine's
$s_{t+1}$, and the $s_{t+1}$ that each model's emitted outcome produces, marked by the engine's judge:
the n-CLU, the matched baselines at 8,000 and 80,000 training steps, the four controls and the two
blind arms. Rows 1--5: per rule family, the first held-out $n$-way transition at seed 0 (rule A has
none at seeds 0 and 1, so its row is from seed 2). Row 6 is drawn by rule among the 199 held-out
transitions at seeds 0--2 on which the n-CLU is correct and at least 4 of the 6 matched baselines are
wrong. Row 7 is drawn by rule among door successes: transitions that open a door with the key in the
pocket and enter a tint of the same colour, on which the model is correct. The n-CLU is correct on
none of its 204 such transitions at seeds 0--2 (no per-transition outputs of the n-CLU exist for
seeds 3--5), so row 7 is drawn from the n-CLU-REL at seed 2, which is correct on 44 of its 55, and
every column of row 7 comes from that run. Both draws are one fixed-seed draw (seed 16), stratified
over rule family and colour. The strip under each $s_{t+1}$ is read as described in
Sec.~\ref{sec:maze-wm}.}{fig:cmazet-families}
