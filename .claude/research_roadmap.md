## v0.5 — CLU AS A GENERAL AI PRIMITIVE (2026-07-21; framing STANDS, evaluation frame SUPERSEDED by v0.6)

> **This block supersedes v0.4's ICLR spine and the 2026-07-20 CAFE pivot.** v0.4's workshop-shorts track is unaffected and still authoritative for those papers. Written at wave-20 launch, before results — **to be edited when w20 lands.**

### The framing (Head, 2026-07-21; Hub sharpenings accepted)
**CLU is a general AI primitive**, at the same level as MLP / GRU / Mamba / attention / DeepSVDD — **not a special case of one**, and a substrate on which special cases (equivariant variants etc.) can later be built. Concretely:
- **Training:** extract features → encode into memory modes → on new data, decide *add to existing basin / open a new basin / trash an old one*. The latent reorganizes toward a well-suited internal structure over samples and epochs — **but this is NOT unconstrained: "learning yes, designed where learning is weak or arbitrary."**
- **The trained CLU** = **`V_θ` (where the basins are — the primary memory substrate)** + **per-address masses and `(q₀,p₀)` (the access structure)**. Both trajectories and settled points carry information.
- **Inference:** query → select basin + mass → roll out → read → **combine** across retrievals. **Lorentz boost** = test-time retry. **Wormhole** = connect two basins otherwise unreachable under the causal limit. **Parallel particles** = multi-basin queries, consolidated downstream.

**Three Hub sharpenings the Head accepted:**
1. **`V_θ` belongs in the learned state and is the primary substrate** — basins live in the landscape; masses and initial conditions are the *access* structure. (Makes Prop 6 land: masses become per-address codebook entries.)
2. **`(q,p)` is two different objects** — a *stored* address is a learned/derived **parameter**; a *query-time* initial condition is a computed **activation**. Never conflate. Current code effectively has only the second, and even that is ~identity.
3. **T3 constrains "reorganizes to the best structure"** — permutation-symmetric regularizers restore *diversity* but **provably cannot choose *assignment***. Design can guarantee structure exists; it cannot decide which item goes where. ⇒ **that decision must be an explicit mechanism or an arbitrary-but-fixed gauge.**

## The unifying thesis (the ICLR spine)
All three verticals are aspects of **one object: the spectrum of the end-to-end Jacobian of a deep conservative network.** Linearizing a CHLU(-Net) at an attractor `q*` of `V_θ`, the eigenvalues of `M⁻¹·Hess(V_θ)(q*)` form a **mass spectrum**:
- **Massless / Goldstone (flat) directions** — exist iff `V_θ` spontaneously breaks a continuous symmetry (Noether ↔ CHLU's conserved quantities ↔ Welling et al.'s Goldstone propagation). No restoring force ⇒ friction kills their momentum but **cannot erase their position displacement** ⇒ *memory that survives dissipation*.
- **Massive (curved) directions** — relax to `q*` under γ with per-mode half-life set by curvature ⇒ **rationed, designable forgetting**. This dissolves CHLU's #1 stated limitation (hyper-stability, paper §D.1).
- **Expanding directions (Lyapunov > 0)** — honest correction to naive "symplectic ⇒ stable gradients": symplectic Jacobians have singular values in (σ, 1/σ) pairs, so deep stacks **cannot vanish but can explode along chaotic directions**. Depth-stability = **spectrum control** (mass spectrum + governor + Lyapunov regularization), not a freebie.

**One-sentence ICLR thesis:** *in deep conservative networks the learned mass spectrum is the memory–compute–forgetting budget — massless modes store, unit-modulus modes compute, curvature rations forgetting; we show how to engineer this spectrum and what it buys on real industrial dynamics.*

### V3 — Deep CHLU-Nets via the CHLU Lattice (ICLR anchor)
- **Architecture decision (v0.2):** lead with the **lattice / many-body** form — one joint separable Hamiltonian with block potentials + sparse coupling `V_c`; everything stays symplectic ⇒ all guarantees survive scaling *by construction*. - **Claims:** trains stably at depth/width **without residuals or normalization** (gradient-spectrum measurements through time/units, tied to V2's theory); expressivity on a composition-requiring task; **energy as calibrated anomaly/health score** on ONE industrial benchmark done properly; Pareto framing vs LSTM/NODE/TCN/PatchTST-class at matched params.
