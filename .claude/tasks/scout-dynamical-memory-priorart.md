## The claim being positioned
CLU is proposed as an **addressable dynamical memory**: information is written into a learned energy landscape during training with a chosen permanence (permanent latch / finite half-life / deletable), and retrieved at inference by **launching a particle of learned mass `m` from a learned initial latent `(q₀,p₀)` and reading its trajectory**. Address = `(m,q₀,p₀)`; retrieval = a rollout rather than a weighted sum. The physics is meant to supply retention guarantees attention has no analogue for (symplectic ⇒ no decay; `μ²` ⇒ tunable half-life; friction ⇒ deletion; mass ⇒ timescale). **The Head's stated ambition: "a competitor to the transformer."**
