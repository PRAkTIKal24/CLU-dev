## The claim to establish (or refute)

> A lattice of SO(2)-designed CLU registers at temperature T, with FDT-correct noise and no governor, is an **XY model on the coupling graph** — and more generally an **O(n) model with n = dim(G/H)**. Consequently the *equilibrium* memory of the array is governed by classical statistical mechanics: a **single** register at T>0 always forgets (uniform on the ring; friction only slows the approach), whereas a coupled array on a **≥2D graph** can have an equilibrium phase that *remembers*.

1. **The reduction (the load-bearing step — verify, don't assume).** Restrict the joint Hamiltonian to the vacuum manifold (each unit at its ring, coset angles θᵢ). Show the position coupling V_c(qᵢ,qⱼ) reduces at leading order to a Josephson/XY exchange ∝ κ·cos(θᵢ−θⱼ) — and **numerically extract the effective coset coupling from a TRAINED 2-unit lattice** (does the learned V_c actually give a cosine, with what κ_eff and what harmonics?). 2. **Identify the dictionary rigorously:** spin stiffness ρ_s ↔ the decay constant **F² = M_ch r*²**; exchange ↔ κ_eff; XY temperature ↔ the Langevin T (state exactly when these coincide). Then **dim(G/H)=n ⇒ O(n) universality class** (n=1 Ising/Z₂ double-well coset, n=2 XY, n=3 Heisenberg) — this is the precise answer to "what do multiple symmetry modes buy?"
3. **The phase diagram by graph dimension.** Single register: equilibrium uniform ⇒ **memory is a transient**, lifetime ∝ γF²/T (reconcile *exactly* with the measured D_θ law — friction slows the approach, it does not change the destination; make this crisp, it corrects the loose reading of "friction preserves"). 1D chain: no LRO, finite ξ(T). **2D: Kosterlitz–Thouless** — predict T_KT in CLU variables and the **universal stiffness jump ρ_s/T → 2/π** (the sharpest falsifiable). d≥3: true LRO. 4. **Prerequisites, stated as blocking conditions.** Gibbs holds only with (i) FDT-correct noise (`langevin_noise="fdt"`, and note `fix-pack-5` is fixing a bug where tied-mass checkpoints violate it), (ii) **no governor** (CM-14: state-dependent γ(H) ⇒ Metropolis-within-annealing, not Gibbs), (iii) γ>0 for thermalization. 5. **The exploration/memory exchange rate (the design principle).** MH/Glauber accepts a discrete structural move (gate open/closed — the genuinely *Ising* degrees of freedom) with prob ~e^{−ΔH/T}, using the CLU's **exact bounded ledger** as ΔH. Derive the exchange rate; show γ enters the erasure but not the acceptance ⇒ **"anneal T to search structure, raise γ to protect the registers."** Also state the CM-3 discipline: this claims *certificates* (detailed balance, exact ledgers), never signal superiority.
