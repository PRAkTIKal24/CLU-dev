## 2. ⛔ The questions that decide it (answer each with a QUOTE + URL, or "not stated")

Per venue, in priority order — **1 and 2 are the bars**:
1. ⛔ **Archival status.** Archival ⇒ the track is BARRED. *"Not stated"* also bars it under Add.4 Ruling 1 — ⚠ **report "not stated" as itself a finding; do not infer non-archival from the absence of the word "archival."**
2. ⛔ **Concurrent / dual submission.** Is work under review at, or accepted by, another venue eligible? ⚠ Note the asymmetry that already bit this program: a venue may permit *subsequent* submission while forbidding *concurrent* — **quote the exact wording and say which it licenses.**
3. **Deadline** (with timezone, AoE or otherwise) and whether it has passed.
4. **Blinding** (double / single / none) and whether it extends to code and supplementary.
5. **Page limit**, and explicitly **whether refs and appendices are excluded** (the Head's standing rule is that limits exclude them *unless stated otherwise* — verify, never assume).
6. **Per-author or per-group submission cap**, if any.
7. **Public posting:** are accepted papers posted publicly (OpenReview or site), with names, and **when**? ⚠ **This is load-bearing here and the estate has it flagged as a gap:** TTCL and PALM both post accepted work publicly on OpenReview and **the timing is not stated by either**. If a destination venue is double-blind and the source venue posts the same work publicly under author names during the destination's review, anonymity is compromised through no fault of the authors. **Report the timing facts for the destination; flag the interaction explicitly.**
8. **Topical fit**, judged against the titles/keywords in §1 — one honest paragraph each. ⛔ A poor fit is a finding, not something to argue around.
