## Context — the reframing that makes this the right next measurement
The Head has rejected the Hub's "CLU's prior is mismatched to a dissipative task" hypothesis, correctly: **CLU is a latent information carrier, not a model of the observed system's dynamics.** Symplectic conservation is about *not losing latent information*, not about the process being conservative. ⇒ **The failure is in how we write information into the latent and read it back out** — the equivalent of running transformer attention on raw pixels with no embedding layer. Currently `φ: data → (q₀,p₀)` is ~identity and `ψ: latent → features` is handcrafted. **This task establishes exactly what those maps are today, so the redesign is grounded in fact rather than in my reading of the code.**
