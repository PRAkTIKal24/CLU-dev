## Item 1 — `GatedCoupling` returns the mean **energy**, not the **free energy** [verified defect]
`GatedCoupling.__call__` computes `sigmoid((t−v)/w) · v = ⟨σ⟩·v`. The physically-correct object for an annealed Ising gate is its **free energy** `F(v) = −w·softplus((t−v)/w)`, whose gradient is exactly the annealed mean force `⟨σ⟩∇v`.

Consequences of the shipped form (defaults `t=1.0, w=0.25`), all verified by the theorist:
- its force `d(⟨σ⟩v)/dv = ⟨σ⟩ − (v/w)⟨σ⟩(1−⟨σ⟩)` **changes sign at `v = 0.8020`** — *inside* the nominally-open region `v < t` — reaching `−0.718`. `V_wh(v)` is **non-monotone: the shipped wormhole repels its own endpoints** over a finite range of separations.
- on an XY bond `v(Δθ)=J(1−cosΔθ)` with `(J,t,w)=(0.1,0.05,0.02)` it flips the effective exchange **antiferromagnetic** (`J₁ = −0.0072` vs the correct `+0.0223`). **A wormhole array built on the shipped gate would frustrate itself.**

**Fix:** return `-width * jax.nn.softplus((threshold - v) / width)` (up to an additive constant). Gradient is exactly `⟨σ⟩∇v`; bounded in `[−w·ln(1+e^{t/w}), 0)`; always attractive; force ∈ [0,1]. **Keep the old expression behind a flag** (`gate_energy_mode="mean_energy"` vs `"free_energy"`, new default `"free_energy"`) — C-9: nothing is silently deleted.

**Blast radius (Hub already audited — confirm, don't re-derive):** `GatedCoupling` is constructed **only** for `wormhole_edges` inside `build_lattice`, and **nothing in the repo calls `build_lattice` with a non-empty `wormhole_edges`** outside `v3-lattice-build`'s smoke. **V1's wormhole experiment does not use this class** (`exp_v1_wormhole.py` imports `channel_spring_coupling` and applies its own held sigmoid gate on the relax residual `R0`, so its force is `g·∇v` with `g` constant — no pathology). ⇒ **no shipped claim is contaminated; this is a latent trap.** State this explicitly in your report, and add a regression test that the corrected gate's force is monotone and bounded in `[0,1]`. `v3-lattice-build` checked the gate's *energy* suppression and never its *force* — **add a force-direction assertion so that class of bug cannot recur.**

## Item 2 — a U(1)-preserving coupling (prerequisite P5)
