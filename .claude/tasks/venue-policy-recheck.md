## Why this exists
The program will submit **five-to-six workshop shorts to NeurIPS workshops simultaneously**, with an **ICLR long submitted before the workshop results are out** and an **NMI long** later. The longs must not be jeopardized. The shorts share a small set of experiments/results/proofs with the longs. The sequencing decisions (arXiv timing for the shorts; whether workshop camera-readies are a de-anonymization/novelty risk for the long) currently rest on **w1-scout policy quotes verified in early July for the PRIOR cycle, before the current ICLR CFP existed** (`.claude/outputs/` venue scout, folded §10 2026-07-05: "non-archival venue-wide; ICLR dual-submission exempts workshop papers"). Every load-bearing policy fact must be re-verified against the CURRENT cycle's primary sources.

## Deliverable — `.claude/outputs/venue-policy-recheck.md`
Six sections, each with **verbatim quotes + source URL + retrieval date** for every policy claim:

1. **ICLR (current cycle) — dual-submission / prior-publication / preprint policy.** Exact current wording on: (a) whether non-archival workshop papers count as prior publication or dual submission; (b) the arXiv/preprint policy for submissions under review (permitted? reviewers instructed not to search?); (c) any anonymity clause touching authors' own public preprints or workshop papers during the review window; (d) whether policy text changed vs the prior cycle's wording the program has on file — quote both if so.
2. **NeurIPS workshop camera-ready publication practices.** For the current NeurIPS workshop landscape, venue classes matching the program's candidates (ML4PS · AI4Science · NeurReps · EBM/generative-modeling-flavored · memory/continual-learning-flavored, as available this year): (a) are accepted papers posted publicly (OpenReview or workshop site), with author names, and when; (b) do any offer opt-out or delayed posting; (c) each workshop's own blinding rule (double- vs single-blind submission); (d) their non-archival statements, verbatim. Where this year's list is not yet final, say so explicitly and report the venue-class norm with examples from the most recent edition — labelled as such, never presented as this year's rule.
3. **Workshop-side concurrent-submission rules.** Can the same or overlapping content be simultaneously under review at ICLR and submitted to a NeurIPS workshop? Quote each candidate class's policy (many exempt/allow; verify, don't assume).
4. **NMI (Nature Machine Intelligence) — prior publication.** Current policy wording on: preprints; non-archival workshop papers; and any constraint relevant to later submitting a long whose supporting results appeared in workshop shorts.
5. **Desk-reject checklist deltas.** Anything in the above that adds to or changes the program's anonymization checklist (metadata, acknowledgments, repo links, self-citation form, supplementary anonymization).
6. **The full venue sweep — every relevant and partially relevant NeurIPS workshop for the shorts, with submission deadlines.** Enumerate the CURRENT NeurIPS cycle's accepted/announced workshops (the official workshop list page is the primary source) and, for every workshop that is even *partially* relevant to any short, report one row: workshop name · URL · **submission deadline (as published, with timezone/AoE as stated)** · page limit · blinding rule · archival status · which short(s) it fits and why (one line). Mark each row **RELEVANT** or **PARTIAL**. Do not filter aggressively — the Head chooses; your job is coverage. A short may map to several workshops. The shorts' topics, for matching:
   - **V1** — test-time compute / adaptive inference on an energy-based associative memory; certified retry/routing mechanisms (physics-of-ML, ML4PS-like; efficient-inference; associative-memory/Hopfield-adjacent venues).
   - **V2** — what a trained recurrent memory obeys: symmetry/Goldstone retention laws, exactly-solvable budgets on trained models (NeurReps-like symmetry/geometry venues; physics-of-ML; science-of-DL).
   - **V3** — composing/scaling a conservative memory primitive: interference firewall, priced communication channel, reversible O(1)-memory training (modularity/systems-for-ML; physics-of-ML).
   - **V5** — predictable, controllable forgetting: decay laws, exact store-level deletion, lifetimes (memory/continual-learning; unlearning/privacy-adjacent; EBM/generative).
   - **V6** — a matched-byte audit of test-time memory dynamics vs tables, uniform protocol incl. our own store losing (benchmarks/evaluation/datasets venues; analysis/science-of-DL; position-paper-friendly venues).
   If the current cycle's workshop list is not yet published, say so explicitly, report the announcement date/mechanism, and give the most recent edition's equivalent list clearly labelled as PRIOR-CYCLE PROXY — never as this year's facts.

## Acceptance criteria
1. Every policy statement carries a verbatim quote, its URL, and retrieval date. No paraphrase-only policy claims.
2. Single-sourced or ambiguous findings are FLAGGED as such (the CM-17 novelty-scope precedent: one WebFetch-sourced quote must be marked).
3. Prior-cycle vs current-cycle differences called out explicitly; if the current CFP/policy page does not yet exist, that absence is itself a finding — never fill the gap with last cycle's text unlabelled.
4. No strategy recommendations — facts and quotes only; sequencing and venue choices are the Head's, advised by the Advisor. Deadlines are reported exactly as published (with timezone), never interpreted into a schedule.
5. §6's sweep errs toward inclusion (PARTIAL rows are wanted); every row's deadline and archival status comes from the workshop's own page, not an aggregator — where only an aggregator has it, flag the row as aggregator-sourced.
6. Output is the single report above, with the standard `## Proposed handover updates` and `## Flags` sections at the end.
