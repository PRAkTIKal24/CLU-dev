## What this task is
The Head has specified CLU end-to-end as an **addressable dynamical memory**: information is written into the learned landscape during training with a chosen *permanence* (permanent latch / half-life decay / deletable), and retrieved at inference by **launching a particle of learned mass `m` from a learned initial latent `(q₀,p₀)` and reading its trajectory**. The address is `(m, q₀, p₀)`; the read is the rollout. **Your job is to determine, mechanism by mechanism, whether this is physically and mathematically possible in CLU — and to say precisely where it breaks.**

**This is the program's thesis.
