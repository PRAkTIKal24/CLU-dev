## 0. Why this task exists, in five sentences
§A13 states the mis-specification plainly: **the settle-deleted launder tests whether inference-time
dynamics beat a table *given the organization* — both arms inherit the same placement — and it has never
tested whether physics-trained organization beats non-physics-trained organization.** A CLU whose write
dynamics organize the latent space well and then reads like a cheap table at inference scores dividend
≈0 **by design**, and ⭐ **that is a FEATURE — no 150–1200-step rollouts per query, an inference-cost win
— not a failure.** **The heavy lifting is at training: the rollout's job is organization.** The Head's
own example is the specification: *an unseen **cat** query landing in wells shared with **dogs** (fur,
four-legs) and far from **crow** wells (wings, feathers).*

## 1. D1 — ⭐ The organization-dividend metric, pre-registered

Define it so it is **computable, falsifiable, and not a relabelling of the tier-i dividend.**
- ⭐ **The comparison:** physics-organized store **vs a matched-capacity non-physics organizer**, scored

  table-like** on both arms — that is the point, and it must be stated in the metric's own definition so

- **What is matched, exactly:** capacity, bytes (two-sided ledger, the **learned-initial-state rule**:
  an init is PARAMETERS, only the per-sequence deviation is STATE, both declared), **identical φ with
  φ-bytes ledgered on every arm**, identical query set, identical read.
- **What varies:** the organizer, and only the organizer.
- ⭐ **The hard specification question you must answer, because it decides whether the metric means
  anything:** *what makes an organization "better", independent of the reader?* Candidate operational
  definitions — argue for one, register it, and say why the others were rejected: (a) **generalisation
  to unseen combinations** at fixed reader; (b) **the reader-class sweep** — the margin must survive
  across a *family* of readers (table, kNN, linear, small MLP), so it is a property of the arrangement,
  not of a matched reader; (c) an **intrinsic geometric measure** (shared-well co-activation aligned to
  latent factors) — ⛔ **weakest, and it must never stand alone**, because §A4.5 forbids naming wells
  semantically: *co-activation / wormhole / shell-position **correlations** with task structure are the

- ⚠ **(b) is the Hub's steer, not a ruling:** a margin that survives a reader class is much harder to

## 2. D2 — ⭐ The cat test, designed to the rules that have actually survived

The vehicle is **the factored store, un-deferred** (§A4.5 / §A14.7): inputs decompose into multiple
feature wells; **wells shared across items** (dog and cat reuse "the same" wells); semantic placement
with basin interaction permitted under the **soft certificate**; multi-particle reads; **composition
between basins as the dividend's natural home**.
- ⭐ **Design the query family to `bprime-fb4-gate` §A3.7's four rules — they are the only rules that
  have survived a +0 B audit, and FB4 killed three of four families that did not:**
  1. the answer is **not recoverable from the table's row order**;
  2. the answer is **not the query itself or a function of it alone**;
  3. the store's operating point is **not** one where the arg-min table sits at the metric's exact
     maximum;
  4. ⭐ **the answer is provably not in the table** — the one generalisable property, and it is what
     `aggregate` had.
- ⭐ **Unseen-combination queries are the design's core:** the test asks for something the store was
  never shown, answerable only by *how* the store is arranged. Register the **construction procedure**,
  the **held-out split**, and — critically — **the proof obligation that the answer is absent from the
  table** (rule 4 is a construction, not a hope).
- ⛔ **Wells are NEVER named semantically.** Write the admissible claim form into the prereg verbatim, so
  C2W5's engineer cannot drift into "the fur well".
- ⚠ **Register the blank/leak controls up front** (collapse mode #4: *reads succeed on a store with
  nothing in it; a 1e-4 address leak makes classification perfect* — N68, blanks 0.992–1.000). A
  compositional family is **more** leak-prone, not less.

## 3. D3 — ⭐⭐ The falsifiers (the reason this task exists at all)

- ⛔ **"The organizer swap shows no margin."** The physics-organized store does not beat a

- ⛔ **"The margin is the reader's."** The margin exists at one reader and vanishes across the reader

  kill-condition before the thing it can kill* is standing doctrine, adopted from C2W3's best judgement

  has **priced** it: matched bytes needs **`S* = (D+2)A_tot/m` items per atom** (**7** at `A_tot = 1`,

  the private-atom fraction, measured degradation on the shared fraction) — **byte-exact deletion is
  never spent silently.** Register the curve's definition.
