## Program principles (meta-thread)

**P1 — "Physics as a design library, not ground truth."** *(Head, 2026-07-04)* We borrow structures from physics (SUSY-like math, black holes under Unruh-like regimes, Goldstone/EFT machinery) for their mathematical beauty and functional value in learning algorithms, with **zero obligation to physical reality**. A virtual black hole in an AI primitive may destroy information even though real black holes (probably) don't.
- *Hub guard-rail (2026-07-04):* every borrowed structure must earn its place with an **ML-measurable benefit** — otherwise reviewers read it as physics cosplay. Discipline: each physics-inspired module ships with (i) its learning-theoretic function, (ii) a falsifiable prediction, (iii) an ablation showing the benefit disappears without it.
- Status: **adopted** (program-level principle; goes in every paper's framing).

**P3 — Minimal structure, maximal learnability.** *(Head, 2026-07-04)* "Enforcing too much structure is bad for learning — we force the AI to think like humans, and we already have humans for that." Design rule: **fix the geometry (symplectic form, causal bound, lever interfaces); learn the content (where memory lives, where trash goes, what masses things have).** Structural choices are acceptable when they are *algorithmic/practical conveniences* (e.g., how many trash regions) rather than semantic impositions — and such choices should be settled by *studies*, not by fiat.
- Status: **adopted** (program-level principle; sits alongside P1's guard-rail).

**P2 — CHLU is a general-purpose primitive with physical levers; applications are frontiers, not identity.** *(Head, 2026-07-04, D2 answer)* Anomaly/fault detection is the *first frontier* (Forgis domain expertise), explicitly not the product. The primitive is defined by its **invariants/levers** (symplectic core, mass spectrum, causal bound c, friction γ, temperature T, energy diagnostics) — current components (wake–sleep, MLP-parameterized V_θ) are **declared placeholders/swappable instantiations** in a WIP design.
- *Hub note:* this is a strong systems-paper move — define the primitive by its interface of levers, present instantiations as replaceable. ICLR intro sells the levers; experiments say "first frontier: fault/anomaly."
- Status: **adopted** (ICLR narrative frame).

## Thread 1 — The Trinity taxonomy & the Trash Region (virtual black hole)

**Raw idea** *(Head, 2026-07-04)*: The three mode classes (flat/massive/expanding) map onto a universal trinity — creation (expanding?), destruction (forgetting), sustenance (memory) — cf. the Hindu trinity. If forgetting is a first-class citizen: physically add a **"trash" region** to the node where information can be *actively destroyed* as needed — a virtual black hole, Unruh-flavored, unconstrained by real physics (per P1).

**Hub development (2026-07-04):**
1. **Mechanism — localized friction field γ(q).** CHLU's current dissipation `(1−γ)p` is *global*: it forgets everything a little. Make γ a function of position: `γ(q) = γ_max · σ((r_h − ‖q − q_c‖)/w)` — a sigmoidal **event horizon** of radius r_h around a trash center q_c, optionally with a potential funnel toward q_c ("singularity"). Outside: conservative dynamics, memories untouched. Inside: infall + momentum annihilation ⇒ practical irreversibility. **Selective forgetting replaces global forgetting.**
2. **Why this solves a real problem:** paper limitation §D.1 (hyper-stability — CHLU can't forget noise without spending global γ and sacrificing memory). Exp B already shows CHLU treats noise as high-energy; a trash region gives high-energy content **somewhere to go and die** while flat-direction (Goldstone) memories, far from the hole, survive untouched. Headline candidate: *"global friction forgets everything a little; a horizon forgets garbage completely and memories not at all."*
3. **Rigorous handle — this is a Lindblad/open-systems structure, not hand-waving:** CHLU-Net = **symplectic (unitary-like) bulk + designed localized non-unitary channels (sinks/sources)** — the classical analog of GKSL/Lindblad dissipators localized in state space. The theorist gets a clean object; no quantum claims needed.
4. **Hawking/fluctuation-dissipation closure — destruction fuels creation:** our Langevin step already ties noise to friction (`√(2γT dt)`). A horizon with γ(q) and local T is a *localized thermal bath*: structure that falls in can be **re-emitted as heat** — i.e., destroyed information recycles as exploration temperature (feeding the sleep phase / generative search). The trinity closes into a cycle: destruction → creation.
5. **Unruh variant (distinct mechanism, same family):** acceleration-dependent temperature `T_eff ∝ |∇V|` (or deceleration) — thermal exploration switches on automatically exactly where the landscape is steep/violent. **Adaptive exploration for free.** Keep separate from the horizon sink; both live in a "thermodynamic modules" family.
6. **Taxonomy mapping (narrative garnish, papers say it soberly):** expanding modes = *creation of distinctions* (amplify small differences; chaotic sensitivity) + thermal bath = creative fluctuations; flat/Goldstone = *sustenance* (transport/memory); massive + horizon = *destruction* (rationed relaxation + targeted disposal). Trinity appears in talks/blog framing; papers use "three-mode taxonomy + dissipative sink."

**Proposed falsifiable experiment (laptop-scale, V2/V3-adjacent):** perturbed sine / lemniscate + injected structured noise; compare (i) global γ, (ii) energy governor, (iii) horizon sink. Metric: **signal-retention vs noise-rejection Pareto curve**. Prediction: (iii) Pareto-dominates.

**Open questions (for Head):** fixed/architectural trash region vs learned placement (the potential learns to route garbage)? One hole or several? Should the horizon's "Hawking temperature" feed the sleep phase explicitly?
- Status: **shaped — candidate for adoption into V2/V3 (roadmap v0.3)**. Cheap, testable, directly attacks limitation §D.1.

**Head's answers (2026-07-04):** (a) **Learned placement** — per P3, don't force structure. (b) **One-vs-several = a study**, not a decision: fixed global hole vs node *spawning local holes as needed*; the choice is algorithmic convenience with likely different training/scaling dynamics. (c) **Hawking re-emission vs pure destruction = an experiment**; if both earn their place, then decide their *positioning* in training vs inference dynamics.

**Hub development round 2 (2026-07-04):**
1. **The friction field should be contrastively learned — same wake–sleep signal as the energy.** Parameterize `γ_φ(q)` (K learnable holes: centers c_k, radii r_k, strengths γ_k — or a small network with saturating output). Train it with the objective CHLU already has: **wake phase pushes γ(q)↓ at data** (memories must live in frictionless regions — a protection term), **sleep phase pushes γ(q)↑ at persistent hallucinations** (garbage attracts friction). Then `V_θ` learns *what things look like* and `γ_φ` learns *what deserves to die* — two fields, one contrastive-divergence signal. Fully P3-compliant: geometry fixed, placement learned.
2. **The spawning study (S1):** arms = {K=1 global hole; K=N local learnable holes; adaptive-K (spawn rule: allocate a hole where sleep-phase hallucination density persists above threshold; prune holes whose γ_k decays)}. Measure: retention/rejection Pareto, training stability, scaling behavior (does adaptive-K track task complexity?).
3. **The re-emission study (S2):** arms = {absorb-only (pure sink); absorb + local re-emission (heat released near horizon → local exploration); absorb + global re-emission (captured energy feeds the sleep-phase temperature budget)}. Measure: generative mode coverage / escape from spurious minima (does recycled heat find more modes?), plus the S1 Pareto to check re-emission doesn't degrade rejection.
- Status: **adopted-in-principle (pending v0.3)** — learned contrastive γ_φ(q) as the mechanism; S1/S2 as designed studies for V2-adjacent short + ICLR ablations.

## Thread 2 — Goldstone multiplicity, pseudo-Goldstones, HEFT/SMEFT

**Raw idea** *(Head, 2026-07-04)*: There are 3 Goldstone bosons — why not use **all of them** to propagate information? If one mode collapses, the others continue (graceful propagation). This is broadly Welling et al.'s philosophy, but they mirror the universe more closely; per P1 we take the math regardless of physical fidelity. The Goldstone formulation partially falls under **HEFT vs SMEFT** frameworks — a Manchester theorist colleague would love to join on that aspect.

**Hub development (2026-07-04):**
1. **Generalize the count — the "3" is the SM's eaten trio** (SU(2)×U(1)→U(1)_EM: 4−1 = 3 broken generators → W±, Z longitudinal modes). The general statement is better than the instance: **#Goldstone modes = dim(G) − dim(H) = dim(G/H)**. So the multiplicity is a **design knob**: choose the symmetry group G of V_θ and the unbroken subgroup H to allocate exactly the number of protected, parallel memory/communication channels the task needs. *Channel capacity by symmetry engineering.*
2. **"One mode collapses, others continue" = pseudo-Goldstone graceful degradation.** Explicit (small) symmetry breaking — by task gradients, data asymmetry, or design — lifts a flat direction slightly: it acquires a small mass ⇒ long-but-finite retention, while exactly-protected channels persist. Quantitative prediction: **retention half-life per channel ∝ inverse of its induced mass²** (theorist to make exact). Memory that degrades *gracefully and predictably* under perturbation — vs LSTM/attention memory which fails unpredictably.
3. **HEFT vs SMEFT = two parameterizations of the latent dynamics (this is concrete, not vibes):**
   - **SMEFT-like:** realize the symmetry *linearly* — equivariant parameterization of V_θ (invariant networks); expansion around the symmetric point in a controlled operator basis.
   - **HEFT-like:** realize it *nonlinearly* — latent coordinates live on the **coset manifold G/H** (nonlinear sigma model layer); kinetic term = coset metric; Goldstones are the coordinates themselves. More general (doesn't assume the "Higgs" completes a multiplet ↔ doesn't assume the memory modes embed linearly in the latent).
   - **EFT power counting ⇒ "an EFT of memory":** higher-order operators = controlled corrections to perfect Goldstone memory. Gives V2's theory a principled expansion instead of ad-hoc perturbation. **This is exactly the Manchester colleague's home turf — natural collaboration surface; could be V2's appendix or a standalone theory follow-up.**
4. **Speculative parked hook — "gauging" the symmetry:** in the SM the Goldstones are *eaten* by gauge fields. Latent analog: promote the global symmetry of a CHLU lattice to a local one with a learned connection on the coupling graph — interactions (V_c terms) as gauge-boson analogs; "eaten" modes = memory converted into inter-unit communication capacity. Beautiful if real; unbudgeted. **Parked** until V2/V3 cores stand.
5. **Differentiation from Welling et al. (running list):** (i) Hamiltonian/energy-space account (theirs: equivariant feedforward/RNN layers); (ii) dissipation interplay — *"friction kills Goldstone momentum but cannot erase Goldstone displacement"* is our claim, absent where there's no friction; (iii) multiplicity/pseudo-Goldstone *engineering* (allocation + graceful degradation) rather than emergence; (iv) EFT organization of corrections; (v) causal bound c on propagation speed of the Goldstone signals (their coherent transport, our light-cone). Web-scout must verify (iii)/(iv) aren't in their 28pp.

**Open questions (for Head):** bring the Manchester colleague in at V2-short stage (co-author) or at the EFT-of-memory follow-up? Which G to demo first — SO(2)/U(1) (single rotational channel, cleanest) or a non-abelian G with dim(G/H)=3 to honor the "use all three" instinct (SM homage, harder)?
- Status: **shaped — strengthens V2's core; HEFT/coset route is a genuine novelty candidate.** Adopt into roadmap v0.3 after Head's calls.

**Head's answers (2026-07-04):** Colleague joins **on V2, minimal capacity**, and will **spar directly with the physics-theorist agent** to solidify the theory (⇒ theorist outputs must be HEP-theorist-legible: proper derivations, standard notation). ## Thread 3 — Lorentz boost as query-escalation in Stacked-CHLU (merges V1 + Shell Jumping)

**Raw idea** *(Head, 2026-07-04)*: The boost was always meant for the *stacked* CHLU: query the smallest layer → if the answer isn't good enough, **first Lorentz-boost within the layer** (semantically clustered re-search in a transformed frame) → only if that fails, **shell-jump** to a deeper/larger layer, and so on.

**Hub development (2026-07-04):**
1. **This repositions V1 from "attention mechanism" to an adaptive-compute retrieval cascade** — and it's stronger: it fuses §E's "Lorentz boosting" and "Shell Jumping" into one mechanism with one control signal, and it's far less scoop-exposed than "attention" (LorentzNet owns Minkowski-metric message passing; nobody owns *energy-gated escalation with structure-preserving retries*).
2. **The confidence signal is energy — CHLU's universal diagnostic.** A query relaxes in shell k; **residual energy** of the settled state (height above the local floor / target energy) = "how badly does this answer fit." Low residual ⇒ answer found; high ⇒ escalate. Calibration of residual-energy-vs-correctness is the first thing to test (and reuses the Exp-B governor machinery).
3. **What a boost concretely *is* in our phase space — a squeeze in Sp(2d).** A per-mode hyperbolic rotation `q' = q·coshζ + p·sinhζ, p' = q·sinhζ + p·coshζ` has det = 1 and preserves dq∧dp: **boosts are symplectic squeeze transformations** — the non-compact directions of the symplectic group Sp(2d) (compact rotations conserve the energy distribution; non-compact squeezes *re-weight relevance across modes*). Quantum-optics squeezing, imported classically. So the intra-layer retry = **line search over rapidity ζ in the Lie algebra of Sp(2d), guided by energy descent** — O(d) per retry, no weight changes, and *provably structure-preserving* ⇒ retries cannot destabilize the state. "The retry that can't hurt you."
4. **The cascade:** shell k relax → [residual > τ?] → boost-retries (cheap, ≤ B steps) → [still > τ?] → shell-jump k+1 (expensive), optionally warm-started from the best boosted frame. This is early-exit/adaptive-computation (BranchyNet/PABEE/CALM/Mixture-of-Depths lineage) with a **physical gate**: escalation priced in energy, retries certified by symplecticity. Timely and differentiated.
5. **Dependency & gate redefinition:** full cascade needs V3's multi-shell lattice. But the **Aug-7 gate can run standalone** on a single CHLU: (a) does residual energy calibrate with retrieval correctness (associative-recall toy)? (b) do boost-retries recover answers plain relaxation misses, at cost ≪ retraining/bigger model? (c) theorist: squeeze-boosts preserve structure (near-trivial now — det=1 argument above) + non-reducibility note vs LorentzNet. Old gate content ("attention non-reducibility") superseded.

**Open questions (for Head):** learned rapidity policy (tiny controller emitting ζ from the query's residual-energy profile) vs gradient/line-search over ζ at inference? Is "semantic clustering" per-attractor (each stored well gets a frame embedding) or global per-shell? Shell-jump = separate trained CHLU at larger dim, or the same lattice with hierarchical coupling (ties into V3 architecture choice)?
- Status: **shaped — supersedes v0.2's V1 definition; gate kept (≈Aug 7) with redefined pass criteria.** Adopt into roadmap v0.3.

**Head's answers (2026-07-04):** (a) **Energy-residual calibration should be a training-time learned objective** — "good enough" is instance-specific (depends on how that CHLU learned that data/modality), so it cannot be structurally imposed; must still generalize off-training-set for deployment. (b) Brainstorm the **actual boosting mechanism first**; the mechanism will dictate the right experiments for learned-rapidity-vs-search. (c) **New intuition: leverage the learned mass matrix to boost different regions differently** — "different particles with different masses within the CHLU (multiple (q,p) pairs)" — ties into V3 scaling. *(Half-formulated by Head's own flag; developed as Thread 5.)*

**Hub development round 2 (2026-07-04):**
1. **Calibration as a margin objective (EBM-native):** wake–sleep already creates an energy *gap* (data pushed down, hallucinations up). Add an explicit learned threshold/margin τ (or margin function) trained jointly so that residual energy *ranks* answer correctness — a contrastive energy-margin loss (classic EBM calibration) + a proper-scoring term. Generalization: τ conditioned on local spectrum/density rather than one global constant (candidate; theorist to formalize). Decision recorded: **calibration lives in the training objective**, not as a post-hoc wrapper.
2. **Boost-mechanism ladder (cheapest-first; answers "mechanism before experiments"):**
   - **L0 — global scalar rapidity ζ, inference-time line search** (zero new params, zero retraining): note that because velocity response is q̇ = p/M_i, **the learned mass matrix automatically converts a single global boost into a region-differential one** — heavy modes barely move, light modes swing. The Head's mass intuition drops out of the existing architecture for free at L0.
   - **L1 — mass-conditioned rapidity profile** ζ_i = ζ·f(M_i) (one learned scalar function; explicit control of the mass-weighting).
   - **L2 — learned rapidity policy** ζ = g_φ(residual-energy profile, state) — the controller; only if L0/L1 show recovery but plateau.
   - Gate experiments now define themselves: L0 on associative recall (does any boost help + is residual energy calibrated?), then climb the ladder only as needed.

## Thread 5 — The mass matrix as the primitive's central budget allocator *(spawned from Head's Thread-3 intuition)*

**Raw idea** *(Head, 2026-07-04)*: use the learned mass matrix to boost different regions differently — like having *different particles with different masses* inside one CHLU (multiple (q,p) pairs); ties into V3 scaling. Flagged by Head as not fully formulated.

**Hub formalization (2026-07-04):** Reframe the d-dimensional latent not as *one particle in d dimensions* but as **a system/gas of d particles, each (q_i, p_i) with its own learned mass M_i** (diagonal M already gives this in embryo). Then one object — M — runs all three verticals:
1. **V2 (memory):** the mode spectrum is `M⁻¹·Hess(V_θ)` — mass *divides* curvature, so M sets each mode's frequency/retention directly. Heavy + flat = deep memory; light + curved = fast scratch.
2. **V1 (boost response):** mass = **semantic inertia** = boost-stiffness. Under any squeeze, light particles reframe strongly, heavy ones barely — so *which regions a boost searches* is learned, not designed (P3-compliant). Relativistically: at fixed |p|, light particles run ultra-relativistic (v→c, governor-dominated), heavy ones stay Newtonian — the causal governor bites per-particle, by learned mass.
3. **V3 (scaling & shells):** build the lattice hierarchy **by mass, not by size** — heavy units/sectors = slow, coarse, long-timescale shells (backbone/identity); light = fast perception/detail shells. The V1 cascade "shell-jump" = moving a query from light sectors to heavy sectors. **A learned timescale hierarchy** (RG flavor: light=UV/fast, heavy=IR/slow).
4. **Extensions:** block-diagonal M = **composite particles** (feature groups sharing inertia — learned "bound states"); "integrating out heavy modes" for coarse-grained inference is literally EFT/renormalization → direct bridge to Thread 2's EFT-of-memory (colleague-relevant). Per-shell c (different causal speeds per sector) noted as a further lever — parked.

**Why this matters strategically:** it consolidates the ICLR story around one learned object — *"the mass matrix is the budget allocator of the primitive: it prices memory (V2), directs search (V1), and sets the timescale hierarchy (V3)."* Candidate for the F5 formalism note's centerpiece.
**Falsifiables:** (i) trained CHLUs develop non-trivial mass spectra correlated with feature timescales; (ii) L0 boosts search light modes preferentially (measure per-mode displacement vs M_i); (iii) V3 lattices with mass-hierarchical sectors beat mass-uniform ones at matched params.
- Status: **shaped — proposed as a unifying element of roadmap v0.3 and F5.** Needs Head's reaction (esp. whether to make "multiple particles" explicit in the lattice design now or post-hoc analyze learned M first).

**Head's answer (2026-07-04): approved with a scaling caveat.** (a) **Interference risk:** in the gas-of-particles picture, all particles share one `V_θ(joint q)` — training must include mechanisms so one particle's learning doesn't **overwrite** another's information in the shared potential. (b) **Mass alone won't scale V3:** the stacked/connected multi-unit ideas remain necessary — **scale by both mass AND size.** (c) Composite particles + EFT-renorm extensions: keep and evaluate.

**Hub development round 2 (2026-07-04) — the interference problem, sharpened:** the collision is in **θ-space**: gradient updates for mode/task A (`∇_θ V` at region A) overlap with what B relies on — single-V catastrophic interference (EBM/continual-learning flavored). Candidate mechanism catalog (→ F5 open-problem section):
1. **Modularity = the hard firewall** (Head's point (b) formalized): separate units each own their V; the lattice's coupling terms `V_c` are the *only* interaction channel ⇒ interference bounded by coupling strength. Multi-unit scaling is not optional — it *is* the anti-interference mechanism. Mass-hierarchy then organizes *within* and *across* units.
2. **Symmetry as an interference firewall** (Thread-2 synergy): modes in different irreps of G cannot mix at linear order — allocate tasks/memories to different irreps and interference is suppressed by the equivariance structure itself.
3. **Block/separable V structure:** composite-particle blocks (`V = Σ_blocks V_b + weak cross-terms`) = explicit non-interaction boundaries inside one unit.
4. **Local parameterizations of V** (RBF/local experts vs one global MLP): local gradient support ⇒ local edits.
5. **Replay** (we already have the buffer — CD's replay is anti-interference machinery in disguise) and **curvature/EWC-style protection** — note the cute duality: Fisher-weighted protection is *"mass for weights"* — inertia in θ-space mirroring inertia in q-space. *(Hub aesthetic flag: parameter-space inertia may unify with the mass story; park for theorist.)*
- Status: **adopted** — M-as-allocator elevated to F5 centerpiece **jointly with** "scale by mass AND size" (modularity as interference firewall); interference section mandated in F5.
