\title{What a Trained Recurrent Memory Obeys:\\ a Mode-Mass Budget for Retention, Latching, and Forgetting}

\author{}

\begin{document}
\maketitle


\begin{abstract}
A physics-structured recurrent unit advances its latent state $(q,p)$ by a damped symplectic velocity-Verlet step of a learned Hamiltonian, and is built to remember. What a \emph{trained} one remembers, of what, and for how long has had no closed-form account. We supply one: a mode-mass budget, verified on trained models at latent dimension $4$ on an $S^1$ testbed, $\le5$ seeds, laptop CPU. Retention half-life scales as $n_{1/2}\propto\mu^{-2}$ in the spectral mass $\mu$ --- a Gell-Mann--Oakes--Renner law, exact to $1.000000\pm5\!\times\!10^{-12}$ over $4.5$ decades --- and saturates at a mass-independent floor past an exceptional-point crossover at $\varepsilon\mu\approx\gamma/2$. The trained potential's symmetry realization sets $\mu^2$; the map's $(\varepsilon,\gamma,T)$ sets what $\mu^2$ buys. Three consequences carry the paper. (i) A recently posted machine-learning lifetime law is the budget's \emph{overdamped face}: run unchanged on our trained units it matches to $\le3\%$, median-consistent with its reported $1.013$, and mispredicts past the crossover in a calculable direction. (ii) A condensate-resolving linear spurion on $8$ trained $SO(2)$ checkpoints makes the three chiral-perturbation-theory objects separately measurable and satisfies GMOR proper, $\mu^2F^2=\delta\Sigma$. (iii) Designed protection beats emergent protection by $13$--$14$ orders of magnitude in $\mu^2$, the retention triad is qualitatively absent in well-trained LSTM/LEM baselines, and the prior is priced: a param-matched free twin fits $\approx15\times$ better at short horizons, then diverges past a measured $\approx700$-step crossing while the unit stays bounded. Designed-testbed results are labeled verification; learned-system results, evidence.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}\label{sec:intro}

A structure-preserving alternative to gated recurrence replaces the black-box update with a physical integrator: a latent state $(q,p)$ advanced by a symplectic velocity-Verlet step of a learnable separable Hamiltonian $H(q,p)=T(p)+V_\theta(q)$, with an optional per-step damping $p\mapsto(1-\gamma)p$ supplying controllable forgetting. Such a unit is built to remember; how much, of what, and for how long has had no closed-form account on a trained model. This paper supplies one --- not a new architecture, but a closed-form account of retention, verified on trained models and compared head to head with a recently posted machine-learning lifetime law and with well-trained baselines. The reference unit is the CLU (Causal Learning Unit), introduced as CHLU in Jawahar \& Pierini (2026); results hold for the class of damped symplectic recurrences, and the exactly-solvable theory verified here is developed in a separate note, cited as \emph{the theory note} (Anonymous, 2026, in the supplementary material).

\paragraph{Nomenclature, not to be conflated.} Two quantities are called ``mass'' and run in opposite directions. The inertial mass $M_i$, the learned diagonal of $T$: larger $M$, slower. The spectral mass $\mu_k$, with $\mu_k^2=\lambda_k(M^{-1/2}\nabla^2V_\theta M^{-1/2})$ at a critical point: larger $\mu$, shorter memory. All retention statements use $\mu$.

\paragraph{Contributions.} (1)~The mode-mass budget, verified on trained models (Appendix~\ref{app:gmor}): the $\mu^{-2}$ law at overdamped slope $-0.985$ against a predicted $-1$, the spectral-mass identity to $5\!\times\!10^{-12}$ over $4.5$ decades, the mass-independent floor and the exceptional point's $\sqrt{h-h^*}$ onset on $5$ designed seeds at dim $4$, and GMOR proper on $8$ trained $SO(2)$ checkpoints. (2)~The head-to-head against Mo's lifetime law on trained checkpoints (\S\ref{sec:mo}), reproduced on Mo's own estimator. (3)~The symmetry-realization taxonomy exhibited by trained models (\S\ref{sec:setup}), with the designed-versus-emergent gap and the price of the physics (\S\ref{sec:price}). (4)~The absence of the retention triad in learned baselines, and the recipe that keeps a designed vacuum broken (\S\ref{sec:baselines}). Designed-testbed results --- invariant potentials, analytic tilts and spurions --- are verification of the theory's exactness; learned-system, training-dynamics and head-to-head results are evidence.

\section{Related work}\label{sec:related}

Neutrality of group-orbit directions is classical (Golubitsky, Stewart \& Schaeffer 1988; Krupa 1990; Rumberger 2001). Closest to us, Mo (2026) proves that a $C^1$ field exactly equivariant under a Lie group has at least $\dim(G/\mathcal H)$ zero Lyapunov exponents tangent to the orbit; we use his diagnostics and his breaking-and-censoring protocol. His result is the \emph{kinematics} of protection, silent on what sets the gap; here the gap is \emph{constitutive}, and Lyapunov spectra are blind to the write semantics --- transport, latch, noise accumulation --- that make a neutral direction a \emph{memory}. His theorem states sufficiency; equivariance is not necessary, the latch being a modulus of $V_\theta$. Constructive equivariant memory (Di Bernardo et al.\ 2025; Keller 2025; Iqbal, Keller, Song, Miyato \& Welling 2026) engineers \emph{which} symmetry a network expresses but does not price \emph{time}: we claim no novelty for choosing a symmetry group or coset, only the price list attached to a chosen structure. Our comparators are LSTM (Hochreiter \& Schmidhuber 1997), LEM (Rusch et al.\ 2022) and coRNN (Rusch \& Mishra 2021a); conformal symplecticity is McLachlan \& Perlmutter (2001), the leapfrog $h<2$ limit standard (Hairer, Lubich \& Wanner 2006), and wake--sleep (Hinton et al.\ 1995) with PCD (Tieleman 2008) the contrastive objective, whose landscape distortion is classical (Fischer \& Igel 2010; Nijkamp et al.\ 2020).

\paragraph{Retention guarantees elsewhere, and what we therefore do not claim.} They are not this family's exclusive property, and the neighbours are occupied ground we cite rather than claim. HiPPO-LegS (Gu et al.\ 2020) has an $O(tL/\sqrt N)$ approximation bound, a polynomial $\Theta(1/t)$ rather than exponential gradient decay and \emph{exact} timescale equivariance with no discretization step to tune, and we have no measurement saying a learned mass spectrum is preferable to it; location-addressable retrieval of dynamical attractors is published (Kong, Brewer \& Lai 2024); the NTM/DNC lineage was already fully soft, with failure modes that transfer (Graves et al.\ 2014, 2016; Csord\'as \& Schmidhuber 2019); tunable permanence is occupied (EDEN, Karuvally et al.\ 2025); UnICORNN (Rusch \& Mishra 2021b) is Hamiltonian, structure-preserving and \emph{has} the gradient-bound theorem we lack; and the modern Hopfield update \emph{coincides with} attention (Ramsauer et al.\ 2021), for a single head with identity projections and $\beta=1/\sqrt D$. Our claim is correspondingly narrow: a physics-structured associative memory in which retention is a designed, per-item property --- permanent (symmetry-protected, $\mu^2\equiv0$ exactly) or of a finite half-life set by $\mu^2$ --- rather than an emergent consequence of a learned recurrence --- \emph{controllability}, which we evidence, not \emph{capability}, which we do not claim against transformers or state-space models. The nearest published neighbour to our write is Titans (Behrouz et al.\ 2025), test-time gradient descent with momentum on an associative-memory loss: the same family of discretized damped second-order dynamics, differing in that our damping and inertia are \emph{read out and priced}, not optimizer hyperparameters.

{\footnotesize\paragraph{Four claims we retire, so that a reviewer's first objections are answered in writing.} \emph{(1) ``Attention and state-space models have no retention-guarantee analogue.''} They do; HiPPO-LegS above is the counterexample, exact where ours is a discrete map at fixed $\varepsilon$ with $h=\varepsilon\mu<2$ a hard stability limit. \emph{(2) ``Retrieval is a rollout, not a weighted sum'' as our novelty.} The move is Kong, Brewer \& Lai's, at capacity $N_c\propto K^{1.08\pm0.01}$ for their one-hot and binary index codings and $1.17\pm0.02$ for their 2D coding; our differences are structural --- neither symplectic, Hamiltonian nor mass-structured there, and the address enters through an index channel rather than as the initial condition of the state. \emph{(3) ``Continuity is our escape hatch from the NTM/DNC lineage.''} False --- both were already fully soft, so the precedent transfers as a warning: of the three DNC failure modes Csord\'as \& Schmidhuber diagnose, key/value entanglement, which they rank first, is architectural in a memory whose address $q_0$ is both the key and the initial condition of the value; de-allocation aliasing maps onto any position-gated forgetting; chained-read degradation is the third; none are measured here. \emph{(4) ``A competitor to the transformer.''} Never claimed, and indefensible at this scale: $\approx1$--$1.6$ bits per emergent register, no win over a trivial baseline on any external benchmark, and formal limits on fixed-size latent state for copying and retrieval (Jelassi et al.\ 2024) that conservation does not evade --- it prevents \emph{decay}, not \emph{capacity}.\par}

\section{Setup, and the two axes}\label{sec:setup}

\paragraph{The map and the budget.} One dissipative velocity-Verlet step with step $\varepsilon$ and damping $\gamma$ advances $(q,p)$ by a kick--drift--kick of $H=T(p)+V_\theta(q)$ followed by $p\mapsto(1-\gamma)p$. It is conformally symplectic, $J^\top\Omega J=(1-\gamma)\Omega$ with $\det J=(1-\gamma)^d$, so memory at each normal mode reduces to one $2\times2$ block ($\mu^2=k/m$, $h=\varepsilon\mu$) with three bands: a latch ($\mu=0$, symmetry-protected) with frozen displacement $q_\infty=q_0+\varepsilon p_0/(M\gamma)$ and infinite half-life; an overdamped register ($0<\varepsilon\mu\lesssim\gamma/2$) at $n_{1/2}\approx2\gamma\ln2/[(2-\gamma)(\varepsilon\mu)^2]$; and an underdamped working memory ($\gamma/2\lesssim\varepsilon\mu<2$) at the mass-independent envelope half-life $2\ln2/(-\ln(1-\gamma))$. The boundary is the exceptional point $h^*\approx\gamma/2$. Trained models are the reference unit's Experiment-D configuration --- dim $4$, hidden $64$, learned-Newtonian kinetic term, $\mathrm{dt}=0.05$, unit circle at $256$ points, $150$ epochs (\S\ref{sec:baselines} for why not the default $1000$) --- in two families: designed, an architecturally-$SO(2)$-invariant $V_\theta$ on $5$ seeds, and emergent, a generic MLP $V_\theta$ on the same data on $3$ seeds.

\paragraph{Axis 1: the symmetry realization of the trained potential.} It fixes each mode's $\mu^2$, a statement about $V_\theta$ alone, in three cells, each realized by a trained model here. \emph{Wigner--Weyl}: unbroken, invariant vacuum, $\mu^2>0$ degenerate within each irrep by Schur --- the contrastive-divergence--eroded vacuum of \S\ref{sec:baselines}. \emph{Nambu--Goldstone}: spontaneously broken, vacuum on a $G$-orbit, $\mu^2=0$ exactly along $\dim(G/\mathcal H)$ directions --- the designed unit. \emph{Pseudo-Goldstone}: explicitly broken, tilted orbit, $\mu^2=\delta\Sigma/F^2$ small --- analytic tilts and the self-broken MLP. Only the \emph{classical, tree-level} Goldstone theorem is used, a statement about a Hessian: if $V_\theta$ is $G$-invariant and its minimiser is not, the orbit consists of minimisers and $\nabla^2V_\theta(q^*)$ annihilates every orbit tangent. No thermodynamic limit, no phase transition, no $\hbar$, and no loops, chiral logarithms, running low-energy constants or anomalies anywhere in this paper. \emph{Axis 2} is the map: $(\varepsilon,\gamma,T)$ fixes what a given $\mu^2$ buys, through the three bands above.

{\footnotesize
\emph{Fine print, next to the claims it qualifies.} \emph{(a)} The latch's infinite half-life is a $T=0$ statement about the deterministic damped map; at $T>0$ the coset coordinate diffuses with $D_\theta=\varepsilon T(2-\gamma)/(2F^2\gamma)$, $F^2=M_{\rm ch}r^{*2}$ --- a \emph{diffusive register} of finite computable lifetime, verified on the trained designed checkpoints, where raising friction \emph{lengthens} coset memory by $3.77\pm0.23\times$ for $\gamma:0.05\to0.2$ on $5/5$ seeds, and holding only under fluctuation--dissipation-consistent noise and a Newtonian kinetic mode, never under the reference implementation's legacy noise default. All \S\ref{sec:results} results are $T=0$. \emph{(b)} By Sylvester's law of inertia a flat direction keeps $\mu^2=0$ under any inertial anisotropy: symmetry protects the write \emph{current}, flatness protects the \emph{register}, and map equivariance is sufficient but not necessary for a neutral memory direction --- our kinetically-broken battery is a \emph{measured} non-equivariant map that latches exactly (Appendix~\ref{app:neg}). \emph{(c)} The \emph{tilt-as-a-designed-lifetime-dial} construction is claimed for designed geometries only, the self-broken MLP being an \emph{observed} instance of the cell and not a dial: in a separate battery of ours on a \emph{learned, multi-atom} store, a breaking coefficient intended as the lifetime dial moved the softest eigenvalue \emph{the wrong way}, and a coercivity term --- not the breaking --- set the ceiling (Appendix~\ref{app:neg}).

\emph{Mandatory flag, travels with every finite-temperature number in this paper.} All such results require \texttt{langevin\_noise="fdt"} --- the fluctuation--dissipation-consistent noise scale $\sigma^\star_i=\sqrt{M_iT\gamma(2-\gamma)}$ --- and a Newtonian kinetic mode (\texttt{newtonian\_identity}/\texttt{newtonian\_learned}). The reference implementation's default is \texttt{legacy}, under which $T$ is not in energy units and none of these laws hold; and in the relativistic kinetic mode no noise scale targets Gibbs at all (Appendix~\ref{app:neg}; \emph{theory note}, Prop-9$'$), so the laws are undefined there. The checkpoints here are \texttt{newtonian\_learned}, so every finite-temperature number here is in scope. Nothing in \S\ref{sec:results} depends on it: all main-text retention results are $T=0$ deterministic rollouts.
\par}

\section{Results}\label{sec:results}

Every result below lives on a trained or learned system. The budget's exactness on the designed testbed is verification, reported in Appendix~\ref{app:gmor}: constitutive identity to $3.2\!\times\!10^{-10}$ over all $70$ tilt rows, GMOR ratio $1.000000\pm5\!\times\!10^{-12}$ across $4.5$ decades, overdamped slope $-0.985$ against a predicted $-1$, floor $27.03$ steps at $\gamma=0.05$, onset exponent $0.5165$ against a predicted $1/2$, and GMOR proper to $1.33\!\times\!10^{-15}$ absolute on $8$ trained checkpoints. With that $2\times2$ block established, to machine precision, as the trained map's own angular block, the sections below ask what it buys.

\subsection{Head-to-head against a recently posted lifetime law, on trained models}\label{sec:mo}

\emph{Evidence: a recently posted ML lifetime law, run on trained checkpoints.} Mo (2026) proves that an exactly equivariant recurrent \emph{flow} has at least $\dim(G/\mathcal H)$ zero Lyapunov exponents along the group orbit --- a lower bound --- and shows that a ``pseudo-gap'' predicts a finite memory lifetime through a single-exponential estimator: the kinematics of protection, silent on what \emph{sets} the gap. Running his exact code-level protocol --- phase $0.35$ rad, threshold $0.2$ rad, his censoring, cap $15000$ steps --- unchanged on the $5$ trained models across $14$ breaking magnitudes gives measured/predicted $1.012\pm0.000\to1.029\pm0.001$ overdamped against his reported median $1.013$, $2.202\pm0.155$ at the exceptional point, and $0.309\pm0.012$ deep underdamped. Mo's law is the overdamped face of our budget: in his regime the two coincide, $\mathrm{corr}(\log\mathrm{pred},\log\mathrm{meas})=0.9987$ overdamped-only, and his predictor fails only past the crossover --- by $\approx3.2\times$ at the deepest trained-model breaking we test, $\delta=4$, our sweep going no deeper underdamped --- in the calculable ballistic direction, with the $2.2\times$ exceptional-point delay spike as the third signature. On the \emph{exact analytic map} the same mechanism continues to $\approx5\times$ further underdamped (theory note, check (k), $\gamma=0.1$); the $\approx3.2\times$ is the trained-model number, the $\approx5\times$ is exact-map only, and we never quote it as evidence about a trained network. This is containment, not conflict: below $h^*$ we recover his reported number; above it the regime structure --- floor, ringing, exceptional point --- is what a first-order flow cannot exhibit and what a \emph{constitutive} gap adds. Nor does it rest on substituting the exact Jacobian gap for his predictor: with Mo's own finite-horizon estimator $\hat\lambda(T{=}128)$ the result holds on his instrument, $\mathrm{corr}=0.9995$ overdamped, meas/pred $0.86$--$1.03$, and $0.30$ at $\delta=4$ against the exact-gap $0.31$.

\subsection{Designed versus emergent protection, and the price of the physics}\label{sec:price}

A generic MLP potential trained on perfectly symmetric data develops \emph{no} near-flat direction: softest emergent $\mu^2=5.1/5.9/5.4\!\times\!10^{-2}$ on $3$ seeds against the designed flat mode $\le2.4\!\times\!10^{-15}$, a gap of $13$--$14$ orders; Mo's own attribution instrument separates the architectures by $15$ orders. Operationally it stores nothing on a \emph{continuum}: $\approx1$--$1.6$ bits on $2$--$3$ washboard minima, a written $\delta$ relaxing completely --- though the damping-optimum retention law itself still generalizes to that arm.

\paragraph{The price of the prior (evidence).} Against param-matched controls --- dim $4$, $150$ epochs, $\pm0.05\%$ params, $3$ seeds, same MLP potential --- the picture is Pareto, not podium. Volume conservation buys bounded (BIBO) dynamics within the coercive-potential / compact-sublevel-set scope ($1.00$ versus $0.33$ for a broken-volume arm), a protected near-flat direction ($\mu^2$ $0.008$ versus $0.122$) and vacuum survival under contrastive-divergence training ($r^*=0.72$ versus $0$); leapfrog buys the latch; and the prior costs raw fit, an unconstrained twin fitting $\approx15\times$ better, $0.0128$ against $0.190$ eval MSE. That cost is \emph{contraction-forbidden} by volume conservation --- the broken-volume arm recovers $\approx2.4\times$ of it precisely by leaking volume --- and is not the causal velocity cap, inactive here. The loan is called at $\approx700$ steps (measured): the twin leads by $1$--$2$ orders to $\approx500$ steps, crosses at $\approx700$ and diverges to $196$ at $5000$, while the unit holds a bounded plateau $\approx0.20$--$0.23$. Its asset is boundedness by construction, not the lowest steady MSE --- broken-volume ($0.14$) and LSTM ($0.13$) plateau below it --- and of the licensed repairs only global damping buys the fit back, a learned friction \emph{field} does not (Appendix~\ref{app:loan}, with the cross-experiment non-comparability caveat its numbers require): paid mechanisms are not interchangeable.

\subsection{Learned baselines, and the recipe that keeps the vacuum broken}\label{sec:baselines}

\emph{Evidence.} On Mo's $S^1$ path-integration family the baselines are trained with a learning-rate sweep, best RMSE kept, and are not strawmen: LSTM and LEM reach train-horizon RMSE $0.18$/$0.23$ rad. Yet under Mo's \emph{autonomous} protocol the structural triad of latch, $\mu^{-2}$ law and bounded drift is qualitatively absent in them: coRNN/LEM/LSTM forget the stored analog phase in $\approx5.6/56/69$ map-steps, drift randomizes past $1.2$ rad, and they are \emph{perturbation-fragile}, a $0.1$ hidden kick collapsing LSTM $69\to2$ and LEM $56\to5$, while the generically-trained unit holds $\approx263$ map-steps with bounded drift ($\approx0.35$ rad, budget law to $+12$--$15\%$) and the designed unit never moves ($5/5$ seeds).

\paragraph{Honest gap.} The load-bearing claim is the qualitative triad, architecture- and compute-independent. The raw $263$-versus-$69$ map-step ratio does not survive compute normalization, and we retire it as a compute claim: per step the Verlet update costs $\approx6.2\times$ an LSTM cell and $\approx3.1\times$ a LEM cell in wall time, $\approx14$--$15\times$ FLOPs, not width-matched, so per unit of retention the unit spends $\approx23.5\times$/$\approx14.6\times$ more wall time than the baselines it out-holds (Appendix~\ref{app:compute}). Lacking native velocity ingestion, it cannot enter Mo's input-driven task-RMSE axis at all, so no task-RMSE was fabricated.

\paragraph{The designed vacuum also needs a training recipe, because the default one destroys it.} Wake--sleep contrastive divergence drives the order parameter $r^*$ to zero, inverting the designed vacuum in $8/8$ runs at the default $1000$ epochs: a symmetry-restoration (condensate-melting) transition in the sense of \S\ref{sec:setup}, the potential landing in the Wigner--Weyl cell with the predicted degenerate $\mu^2$ pair, on a horizon set by sleep-update \emph{frequency} racing the wake clamp, and specific to flat/degenerate vacua. {\footnotesize\emph{Scope clause, travelling with that finding:} our sweep varies chain length only over \texttt{sleep\_steps}$\in\{50,500\}$ at otherwise fixed sampler settings and does not resolve where either sits relative to this model's mixing time, so the finding is \emph{frequency-decisive across the two chain lengths we run}, and is not a claim that chain length is irrelevant to contrastive-divergence learning in general.\par} A $V(\text{data})$ energy anchor holds the condensate up --- $\lambda=100$: $5/5$ seeds, $r^*=0.911\pm0.016$, at the cost of weaker noise rejection and $\approx35\times$ higher wake MSE --- and every headline law of the budget still holds under it at $\approx20\times$ the erosion horizon (Figure~\ref{fig:sf3}): $3000$ anchored epochs, $3$ seeds, GMOR exact to $1.5\!\times\!10^{-12}$ over $4.6$ decades, slope $-0.956$, same floor, exceptional-point onset bit-identical at $0.5165$. The architecture \emph{breaks} the symmetry, the objective \emph{restores} it, the anchor \emph{keeps it broken}.

\section{Discussion}\label{sec:discussion}

{\footnotesize
\emph{Scope box --- limitations and scope choices, stated so no reader has to infer them.} All results are laptop-CPU, dim $4$, the $S^1$ testbed, $\le5$ seeds; the solvable core is quadratic and trained statements are local at a critical point of $V_\theta$, with anharmonic deviations measured as a zero-kick retention bias of $+5$ to $+29\%$, kick-independent for $2/3$ emergent seeds and amplitude-dependent for the softest-$\mu^2$ seed. The head-to-head is on \emph{autonomous retention} in map-application units, and the unit does not enter the input-driven task-RMSE axis. BIBO boundedness is evidenced within the coercive-potential / compact-sublevel-set scope and concerns the autonomous, designed setting measured here --- nothing here claims that symplectic structure buys off-distribution stability in a \emph{trained, input-driven} setting, which we do not test and where a published symplectic architecture already holds the ground with a gradient bound --- and friction never stabilizes a saddle (Appendix~\ref{app:neg}). The symmetry framing is tree-level only. The finite-$T$ diffusion law holds on both the designed channel (verification) and the emergent arm (evidence, $3$ seeds, above $T^\star\approx3\!\times\!10^{-3}$) under FDT-consistent noise in a Newtonian kinetic mode; the one face that does not generalize is the continuous latch register, which is designed-only. \emph{(i) Scale is out of scope by choice:} every number here is dim $4$, hidden $64$, $\le5$ seeds, single-unit, laptop-CPU, and we report no result at larger dimension, wider layers, more units or accelerator scale. \emph{(ii) No external benchmark is won on its own headline metric anywhere in this paper}; the comparisons are diagnostic retention protocols on a synthetic $S^1$ family and matched-parameter ablations, not leaderboard results --- and \S\ref{sec:baselines}'s honest gap is part of the claim, not a caveat to it. \emph{(iii) The substrate scope, once, in our own voice: these laws govern the store; end-to-end performance additionally depends on the encoder, measured separately, with its parameter and state-byte budget ledgered.}
\par}

The budget suggests reading a trained memory as an \emph{effective field theory of retention}: latches as exact flat directions, the moduli of $V_\theta$; registers as pseudo-Goldstone modes of a chosen half-life, fenced as designed-geometry in Appendix~\ref{app:neg}; kinetic isotropy as the price of an equivariant write \emph{current}, not of the register. Named as directions, not results: the $\mu^{-2}$ law, crossover and floor re-measured at latent dimension $\gtrsim64$ and $N\ge8$ interacting units, where mode mixing can break the single-block reduction that makes the budget exact here; the head-to-head on a wider trained-model family; the anchored recipe at longer training and larger width; the equivariant-control wrapper that would put the unit on the supervised task axis; a learned-store re-test of the explicitly-broken cell respecting that fence; the $(\mu,\gamma,T)$ budget cube whose $T=0$ face is \S\ref{sec:results}; and the price list re-derived beyond abelian $SO(2)$, for a torus $T^2$ or the non-abelian $SO(3)\to SO(2)$ coset --- a direction, on which this paper offers no evidence.

\textbf{A reporting caution travels with the isotropization row.} The closed-form charge-oscillation amplitude is an \textbf{on-vacuum-orbit} statement; \textbf{off the orbit only boundedness holds}. The amplitude-to-split ratio is moreover \textbf{not} constant, as the exact envelope requires, since it is linear only to leading order --- so no proportionality constant should be quoted as a law, and we report amplitudes and boundedness, never a ``drift rate''. The design rule attached to mass-tying is correspondingly about the \emph{current}, not the register: \textbf{an anisotropic channel still latches with infinite half-life}; tie the masses for an angle-independent write current, and at $\dim(G/\mathcal H)\ge2$ a degenerate pseudo-Goldstone multiplet, \textbf{not} to save the register.

\textbf{Two reading rules travel with the learned-store row.} \emph{(a)} The breaking coefficient of that battery is an \textbf{explicit-symmetry-breaking magnitude}; it is \textbf{not} this paper's integrator step $\varepsilon$, and the two must never be conflated. \emph{(b)} No sentence in this paper may be read as claiming that a tilt magnitude is a lifetime dial on a learned store, or that a pseudo-Goldstone lifetime can be extended without bound by shrinking the breaking: on a store with a coercive confinement $\alpha\lVert q\rVert^2$ the ceiling is $\Gamma/2\alpha$, and lowering $\alpha$ breaks the write. This paper's own tilt results (Appendix~\ref{app:gmor}) are analytic tilts on \textbf{designed} architecturally-invariant potentials, where the construction is exact; the learned-store scope is exactly what this negative removes. That battery is a \emph{different architecture} from the trained units of this paper and shares none of their training configuration, which is why it is quoted as a scope fence and never as a measurement of the units here. What survives it is the \emph{geometry}: the identity $\lambda_{\min}=\epsilon$ holds in the single-atom geometry in which it was derived, unit-tested to $5\%$, and the registered damped-mode floor $\tau=2/\Gamma$ is confirmed, $5.0$ predicted against $4.23$--$6.58$ measured time units.

\textbf{The sampler row's scope, stated precisely, because the qualifier is load-bearing.} The coded damping substep is an additive Gaussian momentum kick of fixed covariance, so the chain's invariant momentum marginal is necessarily Gaussian-smoothed; for a \emph{relativistic} kinetic term the Gibbs momentum marginal is Maxwell--J\"uttner, which is not of that form (\emph{theory note}, Prop-9$'$ --- a characteristic-function argument needing neither $V=0$ nor linear damping). \textbf{The failure is in the \emph{sampler}, not in the \emph{thermodynamics}}: the configurational equilibrium $\pi_q\propto e^{-V_\theta/T}$ is relativity-insensitive, since the momentum integral factorizes out, so a relativistic unit's equilibrium distribution is perfectly well-defined and the chain simply fails to sample it; a one-line thermostat carrying a randomized, momentum-dependent inertia equal to the relativistic mass repairs it. We therefore never assert that a relativistic unit ``has no equilibrium''. \textbf{This touches no result in this paper:} the trained units here are \texttt{newtonian\_learned} throughout, so $\sigma^\star$ is exact for every configuration we run --- the qualifier is a \emph{scope clause} on a class-level theorem, not a correction to a measurement.

\section{The mode-mass budget verified, and GMOR proper on trained checkpoints}\label{app:gmor}

\emph{Designed testbed with analytic tilts and an analytic spurion, therefore verification of the theory's exactness; $5$ seeds at $150$ epochs plus $3$ anchored at $3000$ epochs, dim $4$, laptop CPU, $f64$ probes, probe-only with no retraining.}

\textbf{C.1 The budget, verified on trained models.} On the trained designed vacuum, whose flat mode has $\mu^2\le2.4{\times}10^{-15}$ and is machine-flat, the constitutive identity is exact: the Hessian-derived spectral mass equals the measured one-step-Jacobian gap to $3.2{\times}10^{-10}$ over all $70$ tilt rows. The GMOR law follows, $\mu^2_{\mathrm{meas}}/\mu^2_{\mathrm{pred}}=1.000000\pm5{\times}10^{-12}$ at every $\delta$ and seed across $4.5$ decades, with fitted overdamped retention slope $-0.985$ ($n=35$; predicted $-1$), saturating past the crossover at the mass-independent floor $27.03$ steps at $\gamma=0.05$. Two second-order signatures confirm a genuine exceptional, defective point. \emph{Retention-metric bifurcation:} envelope half-life and first-crossing time agree to $0.01\%$ overdamped and split by $3.2\times$ at $\delta=4$, so every reported lifetime must name its metric. \emph{Frequency onset:} $\varphi=0$ exactly below $h^*$ ($15/15$ rows, a real eigenvalue pair) and $\varphi\propto\sqrt{h-h^*}$ above, holding down to $h-h^*=2.6{\times}10^{-6}$ on a fine multiplicative grid ($h-h^*\in[-4.2{\times}10^{-3},2.6{\times}10^{-2}]$), fitted slope $0.5165$ against the predicted $1/2$ --- the slight excess is far-field bending --- and prefactor $0.268$ against the theory note's $2\times2$-normalized $0.3247$, a one-line units reconciliation. \textbf{C.2 The damping corollary} --- a supporting result, deliberately not a headline. The same block fixes the half-life's dependence on the damping, and that dependence is \textbf{not} monotone: sweeping $\gamma$ at fixed spectral mass, $n_{1/2}(\gamma)$ traces a V-shape of depth $\approx8\times$ whose argmin sits at, or just below, the exact critical-damping value $\gamma^*\approx2\varepsilon\mu$, on all $5$ designed seeds. Below $\gamma^*$ the mode is underdamped at the floor $2\ln2/(-\ln(1-\gamma))$, which \emph{shortens} as $\gamma$ grows; above it the mode is overdamped at $n_{1/2}\approx2\gamma\ln2/[(2-\gamma)(\varepsilon\mu)^2]$, which \emph{lengthens}; the exceptional point is where the branches meet.
