## 2. The five ratified decisions (Head, 2026-07-29 — all binding)

**2.1 The dynamics dividend is the binding target.**
> The primary benchmark must be a task where the matched-bytes launder demonstrably loses to the full CLU.
Define **dynamics dividend ≡ (full CLU) − (its own settle-deleted / matched-bytes launder)** on the same harness, same bytes, same φ. It is measured on **every** full-system experiment from C2W1 onward. The paper exists when the dividend is positive, robust, and multi-seed on an admissible task (intervention §6 criteria). If no such task can be found at this weight class, that is itself the program's most important finding and is reported as such — it is the null hypothesis of the whole program, stated honestly.

Where the dividend can structurally be positive (a table cannot follow): **(a)** beyond-capacity compression — item-bytes ≫ budget, table must evict verbatim, learned `V_θ` superposes and degrades gracefully; **(b)** non-metric-native queries — composition, aggregation, fuzzy/relational recall, answers computed *between* basins; **(c)** trajectory information — order, ranked alternatives, ambiguity (a trajectory passing near competing wells encodes a distribution over answers; a settled point cannot); **(d)** manifold-valued memories via flat directions. These are the four vision pillars restated as measurable advantages.

**2.2 Benchmark strategy — two tracks, approved.**
- **Track 1 — the internal "memory gym" (development currency; never the paper's primary claim).** A purpose-built streaming suite with capacity pressure, interference, deletion demands, and deliberately non-metric-native queries (aggregate over stored items; relational queries; regime re-identification on revisit; recall under ~3× overload). The launder and the same-keys null are **built into the harness from day 1**. Sole KPI: the dividend. Fast iteration; unexplained wins permitted (explanation-later, never rigor-later).
- **Track 2 — the public fight (the paper's primary claim).** **Primary: bounded-state long-context sequence modelling** — CLU as the recurrent memory of a streaming model vs **real Mamba** (standing rule), GRU, sliding-window attention, and the modern neural-memory family (**TTT / Titans-class test-time memory, fast weights**). Concretely: the MAD/zoology-style synthetic memory suite (community currency) + one small-scale real-data run (enwik8 or WikiText) at **matched parameters AND matched state-bytes**. **Fallback:** online prediction on non-stationary streams with regime revisits, judged on retention + adaptation at matched bytes — admissible because the difficulty is memory management over time, **never** because the data is "physics-like" (that framing stays banned; the CLU is a latent information carrier, not a model of the data's dynamics).
- **The compute constraint is faced, not hidden:** 150–1200 Verlet steps per read decides feasibility at sequence scale. Mitigations: memory operations at **chunk granularity** (as Titans-class memories do — fair), and compute-adaptivity as a **feature**: the accuracy-vs-Verlet-steps **anytime curve is a signature figure** no baseline can draw (the trilemma's third corner).

**2.3 The C1W27 scope (w26 decision queue, ruled).** d-sweep at m=4 (d=6, d=8) — **YES, the wave headline**; CIFAR entry run — **NO** (5 h buys a pre-registered negative); `pass_metric` default → `decode` — **YES** (correctness fix, not a promotion); P2 waitlist — **YES** (takes deletion from "exact below capacity" to "exact"). **2.4 Placeholder policy — approved.** For end-to-end trainability, standard machinery replaces placeholders wherever one blocks progress: **implicit/DEQ-style gradients at settled points** (a settle is a fixed point — differentiate through the equilibrium, not the unroll), truncated backprop over trajectory reads, plain Adam on φ/ψ/controller. **Wake–sleep is repositioned as the consolidation phase** (offline maintenance: re-packing, decay enforcement, gate re-calibration) — more defensible and truer to what sleep does. The primitive is the dynamics + store + control; the trainer is replaceable infrastructure, and the paper says so as a strength.

## 3. Synthesis doctrine (how the full CLU gets built without collapse)

1. **Staged activation, not big-bang.** Every lever starts in its known productive band (26 waves are the map). The 13 anti-collapse modes (intervention §5) are **runtime monitors that fail loudly**, not losses. Levers are freed one at a time *inside the running full system* — isolation is permitted only as a diagnostic within it (intervention §8.1). Side benefit: this manufactures the ablation table reviewers demand.
2. **Designed action space, learned policy.** The controller's verbs (admit, place, evict, decay, route, retry, stop) and guards are **designed**; when and how hard to pull them is **learned**. This is the program's own strongest cross-wave finding (designed mechanism + learned use beats learned-everything) applied at the controller level. Do not relearn w20's lesson (free learning erases design) up here.
3. **Fixes-as-ammunition.** The never-tested arsenal (wormholes, Lorentz-boost retries, flat-direction/manifold storage, trash regions `γ_φ(q)`, multi-particle reads) enters **as fixes deployed when the corresponding monitor fires on the real task** — never as isolated studies. This is how the never-tested list gets tested without violating the prohibitions.
4. **Epistemics unchanged.** Pre-registration with declared falsifiers, multi-seed before any paper number, launder + same-keys null on every performance claim, negatives filed, quote-the-curve, §7 dial declaration at scoping. Performance-first means explanation-later, never rigor-later.
