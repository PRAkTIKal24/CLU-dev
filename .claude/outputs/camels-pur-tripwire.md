## 11. Limitations / confounds
- **The verdict is against the *published* region rule but our own execution of it.** The rule is
  pinned from the authors' code and cross-validated four ways (§2.2–2.3), but Li 2025 does not publish
  a fold file. Residual risk is small and bounded by §6's 0.0085 spread.
- **Daymet-only.** The binding reference (P21) is an NLDAS row and P20/P29/P30/P32 are maurer rows;
  P10 (LSTM+δHBV Daymet) is the forcing-matched primary. Annotated per row.
- **Feng 2023's PUR rows use 1989–1999**, not our 1995–1999. Same regions, different window.
- **E is a median.** The winning arm's *mean* NSE is **−0.373** with **112 of 531** basins at NSE ≤ 0 —
  far more heavy-tailed than the LSTM references. In-convention (the venue's metric is the median), but
  worth stating.
- **The DAY W1 arm is a same-day forcing → specific-discharge lookup.** It is legal here (forcings are
  available at ungauged sites) and it is the honest floor of the venue. Anyone quoting a PUR result
  should quote it: *"a 1.18 MB same-day forcing lookup scores 0.274 here."*
- **The k-means condensation cap `KMEANS_LMAX = 5000`** is inherited from the PUB harness and is a
  declared limit; it affects only large-L DAY points, which are laddered by random selection instead.
- **Concurrent-donor payloads remain legal** under a spatial holdout, as in PUB. On PUR they are worth
  almost nothing (0.106), so the question that mattered for PUB is moot here.
