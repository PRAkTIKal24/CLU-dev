                   012              Aworld model that is to be trusted with requests it has never seen needs three
                   013              things at once: its outputs must be valid under an exact judge, it must compose
                   014              held-out combinations of factors from factors it has seen, and it must be able to
                   015              forget specific training rows exactly. Compositional generation is a crowded area
                   016              and exact unlearning is an established one. We work at the level of a single naive
                   017              unit and compare it only with other naive units (an MLP, an LSTM, a transformer
                   018              and a continuous-input MLP): we do not claim the best compositional-generation
                   019              or unlearning system, and systems built on top of units, such as sharded retraining
                   020              or compositional diffusion, are out of scope. We study the simplest object that
                                    has all three properties, the Naive CLU: a label-free store of per-well principal
                   021              subspaces computed on the latents of a frozen encoder, read by a request-indexed
                   022              address book and rendered by a frozen decoder, whose content is derived from
                   023              a retained sufficient statistic. Deleting rows downdates the statistic, not the trun-
                   024              cated factors, so the store after deletion equals the store fitted without those rows.
                   025              On a Shapes3D value hold-out at K⋆ = 32 wells, the finest partition on a vali-
                   026              dation plateau (K = 5...32) that three seeds do not resolve, the store composes

                   030              the store’s own 131,072 rows, each also trained for ten times its selected optimiser

                 057     1.1  POSITIONING: A NAIVE UNIT, COMPARED WITH NAIVE UNITS
                 058
                 059     The Naive CLU is a naive AI unit: one model with no system built around it, in the sense in which
                 060     an MLP,anLSTMoratransformerisaunit. Wecompareitonlywithothernaiveunits,anMLP,an
                 061     LSTM,atransformer and a continuous-input MLP, matched on the read path (§2). We do not claim
                 062     the best compositional-generation system or the best unlearning system. Systems built on top of
                 063     units, such as sharded retraining, compositional diffusion and physics-informed variants of an MLP,
                 064     are out of scope by definition; each is cited in one sentence below, with the unit/system distinction
                 065     stated.
                 066     Generating held-out combinations of factors is an old and active problem (Du et al., 2020; Liu et al.,
                 067     2022). The multilinear line (Tenenbaum and Freeman, 2000; TensorFaces, Vasilescu and Terzopou-
                 068     los, 2002; Vlasic et al., 2005; MLC-VAE, 2020 (Georgopoulos et al., 2020)) factorises a data tensor
                 069     organised by every label combination and renders linearly; the frozen-generator line (GANSpace,
                                ¨  ¨
                 070     2020(Harkonenetal.,2020);TCA,2021(Oldfieldetal.,2021);PandA,2023(Oldfieldetal.,2023),
                 071     andits 2024journalversion(Oldfieldetal., 2024)) finds editable directions in a pretrained generator
                 072     without labels and without an address book. We do not claim either half. In particular we do not
                         claimmultilinear or bilinear factored generation, a request-indexed tensor head, tensor-product label
                 073     conditioning, or the downdate primitive, which follows in one line from the summation form of Cao
                 074     and Yang (2015) because a covariance is a summation.
                 075     Exact unlearning is also established, for k-means (Ginart et al., 2019), for bilinear matrix comple-
                 076     tion (Xu et al., 2023), and for ridge heads on frozen backbones (Quan et al., 2026), where the most
                 077     recent work restricts itself explicitly to ridge regression and excludes principal-component, eigen-
                 078     decomposition and generative models. The nearest generative attempts are approximate (Kong &
                 079     Alfeld, 2022): feature unlearning for pretrained GANs and VAEs (Moon et al., 2024) unlearns a
                 080     feature rather than rows and fine-tunes the generator; data redaction from pretrained GANs (Kong
                 081     &Chaudhuri, 2023) states that redaction is a different task from deletion; GAN unlearning by sub-
                 082     stitution (Sun et al., 2025) is approximate by construction.
                 083     Asystematic screen of both literatures records what we could find. A forward-citation crawl (Priem
                 084     et al., 2022) over every title and abstract citing the canonical works of either side screened ap-
                 085     proximately 4,050 unique works: 3,038 citing the generation-side anchors, 759 citing the deletion-
                 086     side anchors, 254 from 21 orthogonal queries that cite neither, and about 100 from a final targeted
                 087     sweep. Deletion language appears in 10 of the 3,038 generation-side works (0.3%), seven of them
                 088     false positives and none exact; no work among the 3,038 combines frozen-decoder, tensor-head and
                 089     label-index language (0 of 3,038); the highest number of the six claim atoms reached by any single
                 090     work is 4 of 6; and of the 759 deletion-side works none combines principal-subspace, generative
                 091     and frozen-model language. The orthogonal query that asks the question directly, compositional ∧
                 092     generative ∧ exact deletion, returns zero works on the screen. These are the screen’s counts, not
                 093     a claim that no such model exists: abstract-level screening cannot see a match buried in a method
                 094     section, and indexing of preprints younger than about three months is incomplete (§5). Three fam-
                 095     ilies of systems built on top of units are out of scope, one sentence each. SISA training (Bourtoule
                         et al., 2021) is a system that makes any unit, a generator included, exactly unlearnable by split-
                 096     ting its data into shards and retraining the unit on the shard that held the deleted rows, which reads
                 097     that shard’s retained data; we compare with the unit it wraps, whose only exact route is the retrain
                 098     of §3.4. Compartmentalized diffusion models (Golatkar, Achille, Swaminathan and Soatto) are a
                 099     system that trains separate diffusion models on separate data sources, composes them at inference
                 100     and forgets a source by dropping its model, so what is composed is models of data sources, not
                 101     held-out combinations of factor values within one unit. Physics-informed variants of an MLP, such
                 102     as physics-informed neural networks (Raissi, Perdikaris and Karniadakis, 2019) and Hamiltonian
                 103     neural networks (Greydanus, Dzamba and Yosinski, 2019), are systems that add a physical loss or
                 104     structure to an MLP unit, and the rival we compare with is the plain MLP unit.
                 105     One distinction is ours and worth a sentence. The decremental-PCA line (Hall, Marshall and Mar-
                 106     tin, 2002, and successors (Jin et al., 2014; Lee, 2021)) subtracts deflated eigenspaces; its authors
                 107     describe the result as approximating the refit, report that subtraction tends to instability, and give no
                         rigorous error analysis. Our route is exact for the opposite reason: downdate the statistic, not the
                                                                    2
                 108     truncated factors. The full, untruncated second-moment statistic is downdated by subtraction and
                 109     the content is re-eigendecomposed afterwards, so no truncation error ever enters the subtraction.

                 111     1.2  WHYTHETHREEPROPERTIESMATTER: WORLD MODELS NEED ALL THREE AT ONCE
                 112
                 113     Aworldmodel(Ha&Schmidhuber,2018;Hafner et al., 2025) is useful to the extent that it can be
                 114     asked for states and transitions it has not seen (Valevski et al., 2025), and trusted with the answer.
                 115     Wename three properties and use them throughout. Rule validity: an output is valid if an exact
                 116     judge, integer arithmetic with no tolerance, accepts it (Vafa et al., 2024); approximate similarity to a
                 117     training item does not count. Composition: the model produces valid outputs for held-out combina-
                 118     tions of factor values that it has seen only separately (Zhao et al., 2022). Exact deletion: after a set
                 119     of training rows is deleted, the deletable object equals, to floating-point roundoff, the object fitted
                 120     without those rows. The three pull against each other: a lookup table deletes exactly but composes
                 121     nothing it has not stored; a learned generator composes but can delete exactly only by retraining
                 122     (the whole model, or a shard of it (Bourtoule et al., 2021)); and approximate unlearning (Guo et al.,
                 123     2020; Golatkar et al., 2020) is approximate by construction, not exact (§3.4). The object we study
                 124     makes the three properties of one store, and we measure, row by row, where each holds and where
                 125     it does not.

                  305     The deletable object is the single design: the store, read and ledger of §2 on the Shapes3D
                  306     value hold-out (Montero et al., 2021; Schott et al., 2022), with the partition fixed on validation
                  307     by a rule frozen before the sweep, K⋆ = max{K : v(5) − meanseedsv(K) ≤ 0.02}, over
                  308     K ∈ {5,8,10,12,16,20,24,28,32}. Every swept K satisfied the rule, so K⋆ = 32, the finest
                  309     partition swept; the sweep range was not extended to manufacture a crossover. That is the frozen

                 921     5.3  THE THEORY WE CARRY: THE LATENT DYNAMICS MODEL CLASS
                 922
                 923     Wecall the class of objects that this store is the static corner of a Latent Dynamics Model: a store
                 924     whose content is read by relaxing a latent state under a designed energy (Hopfield, 1982; Krotov &
                 925     Hopfield, 2016; Ramsauer et al., 2021; Hoover et al., 2023; Kong et al., 2024), of which the Naive
                 926     CLUisthememberwiththerelaxationswitchedoff. (Thephraseisusedinreduced-ordermodelling
                 927     and in model-based reinforcement learning for a learned surrogate of an environment’s dynamics;
                 928     here the dynamics are the read, not a model of the data.) Its laws are this paper’s own theory, each
                 929     with a status label and rider (further laws in Appendix P).
                 930     Twolawsmeasuredonthisstudy’sownstore(CONFIRMED [THIS STUDY]). Switchedon,the
                 931     field store’s relaxation obeys a curvature–retention relationship whose prediction is a time, not a
                 932     score: with nothing fitted, it predicts the settle half-life to a median factor of 1.105 on 49 held-out
                 933     corners and three seeds (bar 0.10 on the log error; 0.0433 / 0.0435 / 0.0431), and beats a one-
                 934     parameter form fitted with sight of the held-out measurements on 49 of 49 corners on every seed;
                 935     oneofsixpre-registered criteria (the V-curve argmin) misses on all three seeds, and the law predicts
                 936     where its simplest form stops. Deletion commutes with scheduled decay on a learned store, 0 bytes
                 937     differing in a 2,054,866-byte image, while the stronger claim that the post-delete store equals the
                 938     never-inserted one is REFUTED (65.17% to 65.39% of the image differs, 83.5% of it because the
                 939     address book and coupling table are fitted on the pool). These are dynamics results about our own
                 940     store. They occupy no row of the evaluation, they beat no model, and we make no capability claim
                 941     from them; no arm of ours fails the retention law, so it distinguishes nothing. The full statements,
                 942     criteria and misses are in Appendix O.
