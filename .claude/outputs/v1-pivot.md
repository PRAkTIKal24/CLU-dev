## Findings/results (full run; mean±std over 5 seeds)

**1. Calibration transfer — the pivot's core claim: SURVIVES, decisively.**
| | kv=16 | kv=24 | kv=32 | pooled all levels |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| base / full / Hopfield acc | 0.842±0.028 / 0.847±0.037 / 0.995 | 0.433±0.042 / 0.525±0.032 / 0.999 | 0.258±0.062 / 0.280±0.037 / 0.983 | — |
| fidelity | 1.000 | 0.944 | 0.717 (storage-limited) | — |
| within-model AUROC(R→wrong) | 0.750±0.052 | 0.693±0.075 | 0.545±0.055 | — |
| pooled raw-R AUROC | 0.690±0.065 | 0.676±0.049 | 0.560±0.077 | **0.431±0.038** (per-seed 0.365–0.471, all < 0.5) |
| pooled **calibrated** AUROC | **0.912±0.014** | **0.785±0.035** | **0.715±0.045** | **0.869±0.015** (0.845–0.889) |

Replicates & fixes the gate run's 0.330 pathology across all 5 seeds: raw R pooled across models is *anti*-ranked; the training-time per-model head makes it strongly deployable. n_wrong0 per cell: kv16 17–26, kv24 74–90, kv32 84–106 — error bars are real now (gate run had n=8).

**2. Compute allocation (powered "8×" result): survives at easy/moderate difficulty; correctly refuses to save at hard.** Learned operating point (τ fixed at write time, p_exit=0.5 — no post-hoc sweep):
- kv16: **0.894±0.021 @ 629±60 steps** vs always-full 0.847±0.037 @ 3000 → **4.81±0.44× savings AND +4.7 pts above always-full** (and above always-small 0.842) — the gate beats *both* endpoints: exits early where extra relaxation degrades, escalates where it helps.
- kv24: 0.547±0.039 @ 1919±82 (1.57±0.07×; +2.2 pts vs full). kv32: 0.286±0.037 @ 2636±127 (1.14±0.06×; +0.6 pts).
- Clean claim: **the learned gate never pays full price for less accuracy at any level**; savings scale with how much of the workload is confidently easy. Compute figure: `exp_v1_calibration_compute.png`.

**3. Abstention head-to-head vs Hopfield: LOST (the task's kill-criterion, reported honestly).** Hopfield acc 0.983–1.000 ⇒ its AURC ≈ 0.000–0.001 under naive MSP, naive logit-margin, *and* calibrated confidence, at every level; cov@risk5% = 1.00 everywhere. Best CLU AURC (calibrated R+margin): 0.030±0.009 / 0.330±0.041 / 0.578±0.094. An imperfect memory cannot out-abstain a near-perfect always-answering one — on MQAR at this scale the comparison is unwinnable regardless of gate quality, because the baseline has almost no risk to manage. **The V1 short cannot use "abstains better than Hopfield" as a claim at this task/scale.** What stands *within*-CLU: calibrated-R beats raw-R everywhere (AURC 0.061/0.409/0.655 vs 0.084/0.429/0.721; pooled 0.244±0.018 vs 0.517±0.048); calibrated R+margin ≈ best available signal (pooled AURC 0.212±0.017); cov@risk10% kv16 = 0.90. Post-escalation abstention beats base-stage at hard levels (deployed-head AURC full-budget 0.234 vs 0.330 @ kv24; 0.514 vs 0.578 @ kv32) — escalation improves the abstention frontier, not just accuracy.
**4. "Does energy add information over margin?" — essentially no at this scale.** ΔAUROC(calib R+margin − calib margin): kv16 −0.004 (signs mixed 2/5 −), kv24 +0.024 (4/5 +), kv32 +0.009 (4/5 +). Energy is a *valid* gate feature (calibrated-R alone works) but adds ~nothing over the readout margin; weak positive trend as difficulty rises. Reportable either way per the task — this is the "doesn't" branch, quantified.

**5. LTT/CALM wrapper (stretch, delivered): 100% empirical validity — 30/30 (level,seed,ε) cells, zero guarantee violations despite the probe→exact-cue shift.** kv16: certified 5/5 at both targets, coverage 0.647±0.063 @ ε=0.05 (eval risk 0.030), 0.767±0.040 @ ε=0.1 (risk 0.053). kv24: 3/5 @ 0.05 (cov 0.035, risk 0.000), 5/5 @ 0.1 (cov 0.067, risk 0.011). kv32: 0/5 certified ⇒ abstain-everything, vacuously valid. Graceful degradation: when the memory is bad, the certificate honestly refuses to promise.

**6. Calibration quality (ECE):** our heads 0.085–0.157 per level (pooled 0.100±0.021), *under-confident* direction (reliability curve above diagonal — the safe side for abstention; figure `exp_v1_calibration_reliability.png`). Hopfield MSP 0.002–0.016 (trivially calibrated because always right). ECE reported only for probability-valued signals — raw margins/energies aren't probabilities (documented deviation from "ECE of each signal").

**Honest read for the Hub (mine; decision is yours):** the pivoted headline — *calibrated energy-gated compute allocation on conservative memories* — survives powered statistics in its three load-bearing components (cross-model calibration transfer 0.43→0.87; learned-point compute allocation that never loses to always-full and saves ~5× on easy workloads; distribution-free risk certificates that were never violated). The anti-Hopfield abstention framing dies and should be dropped or reframed: Hopfield's near-perfection at these kv makes "we abstain better" unwinnable; the defensible positioning is *risk-controlled deployment + compute rationing of an imperfect associative memory* (with Hopfield's dominance stated plainly), or the head-to-head must move to a regime where the baseline actually errs (much larger kv/vocab, noisy cues, capacity-stressed Hopfield). Also: energy ≈ margin as gate feature here — the short should claim the *gate mechanism + certificates*, not energy's superiority as a signal.
