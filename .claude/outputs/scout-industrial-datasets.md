## (2) Top-3 justifications + RUL pick + negative-control pick

**#1 voraus-AD.** Strongest criterion-1 fit in the pool: a robot manipulator *is* a Hamiltonian system (rigid-body kinetic energy + gravity/actuation potentials), and its 12 anomaly types are departures in exactly the quantity CLU natively measures — energy residual above the learned floor. Unsupervised normal-only protocol matches wake–sleep with zero adaptation. Purpose-built, peer-reviewed (accepted T-RO), one clear published bar (MVT-Flow, +6.2% AUROC over prior baselines) rather than a decade of protocol soup. Trivially A100-feasible at 500 Hz. Bonus: continuity with FactoryBench/FactoryWave. Risks: NC license, thin comparison field — mitigated by running #2/#3 alongside.

**#2 SKAB.** "Our attractor experiments, but real": physical rotating pump loop whose labeled faults (cavitation, shaft imbalance, reduced motor power, partial valve closure) are textbook energy-signature excursions, directly testable against the Exp-B energy-governor machinery. Tiny size → the *iteration* benchmark: every ablation (γ_φ trash-field, mass-spectrum, governor) runs here cheaply. Public leaderboard tops out at F1=0.78 — real headroom, unlike saturated benchmarks. Both outlier and changepoint labelings. Risk: smallness limits headline weight → mechanism-demonstration role; voraus-AD carries the headline.

**#3 Tennessee Eastman (Rieth CC0 edition).** The review-proofing pick: after the benchmark-hygiene scandal (below), the strongest defensive property is *unimpeachable labels* — TEP's are perfect by construction (500 seeded simulation runs per class, CC0, single canonical Dataverse DOI). Scale/breadth benchmark (52 vars, 20 fault classes incl. canonically-hard 3/9/15); process-industry audience. Weakest criterion-1 fit of the three (chemical, dissipative, simulated) — which is *useful*: it brackets where CLU's mechanical priors matter vs. don't.

**RUL pick:** **N-CMAPSS** primary (real flight envelopes, run-to-failure, the field's own C-MAPSS successor; DS02 protocol from PHM'21 challenge), legacy **C-MAPSS FD001–FD004 reported for comparability only** (SOTA RMSE ≈ 11.0–11.4 / 14–16 on FD001/FD004). **XJTU-SY** bearing-RUL secondary (true run-to-failure; connects to Paderborn diagnosis story).

**Negative-control pick:** **SMD** — server telemetry (CPU/memory/network) has no conservation/symplectic structure; if CLU's energy-anomaly signal still "wins" there, we're measuring benchmark pathology, not physics benefit (P1 falsifiability rule). Use SMD *as curated inside TSB-AD-M* to dodge its own triviality criticisms; score with VUS-PR.

## (3) Evaluation-protocol recommendations (feeds F2)

The 2021→2026 TSAD evaluation literature has converged; violating this is now a desk-reject-grade reviewer flag:

1. **Never use point-adjust (PA) F1.** Kim et al. (AAAI 2022, arXiv:2109.05257): PA "has a great possibility of overestimating detection performance" — **a random anomaly score can become SOTA** under PA; untrained models match published methods even without PA. All classic SMD/SMAP/MSL/SWaT SOTA tables (OmniAnomaly, Anomaly-Transformer lineage) are PA-inflated; never compare at face value.
2. **Primary metric: VUS-PR** (threshold-free, lag-tolerant), per TSB-AD (Liu & Paparrizos, NeurIPS 2024 D&B): "we identify the most reliable and accurate measure, namely, VUS-PR." Secondary: range-aware/event-wise metrics (affiliation, eTaPR-class) + plain AUROC/AUPR for continuity. Report per-dataset, never one grand mean.
3. **Dataset hygiene:** Wu & Keogh (IEEE TKDE 2021) failure modes — triviality, mislabeled ground truth, unrealistic anomaly density, run-to-failure bias. SWaT/WADI/SMAP/MSL each documented as afflicted (SWaT: constant features + one anomaly ≈ 65% of anomalous timesteps; WADI: 33 redundant + 8 broken features). Our three picks are maximally robust to this attack (purpose-built / physical rig / simulated-perfect labels).
4. **Mandatory simple baselines.** Quo Vadis (Sarfraz et al., ICML 2024): deep TAD models "effectively learn linear mappings"; added complexity buys "very little improvement." TSB-AD concurs: statistical methods (IForest, LOF, KNN, PCA, MSET) often beat deep nets; neural nets help mainly in multivariate cases; foundation models on point anomalies. **Every CLU results table includes PCA/IForest/LOF-class baselines.** This also serves our Pareto-not-podium framing: energy interpretability + certificates at matched accuracy survives a linear-baseline tie.
5. **Leakage-safe splits for machinery** (Hendriks et al. 2022 on CWRU): split by physical unit (bearing/run), never by window; report cross-operating-condition generalization.
6. **Harness reuse:** TSB-AD (Apache-2.0) and TimeSeAD provide loaders + metric implementations — F2 should wrap, not reimplement, VUS-PR.
