\begin{abstract}
A world model that is to be trusted with requests it has never seen needs three things at
once: its outputs must be \emph{valid} under an exact judge, it must \emph{compose}
held-out combinations of factors from factors it has seen, and it must be able to
\emph{forget} specific training rows exactly. Compositional generation is a crowded area
and exact unlearning is an established one. We work at the level of a single \emph{naive unit} and
compare it only with other naive units (an MLP, an LSTM, a transformer and a continuous-input MLP):
we do not claim the best compositional-generation or unlearning system, and systems built on top of
units, such as sharded retraining or compositional diffusion, are out of scope.
We study the simplest object that has all three properties, the \emph{Naive CLU}: a
label-free store of per-well principal subspaces computed on the latents of a frozen
encoder, read by a request-indexed address book and rendered by a frozen decoder, whose
content is derived from a retained sufficient statistic. Deleting rows downdates the
statistic, not the truncated factors, so the store after deletion equals the store fitted
without those rows. On a Shapes3D value hold-out at $\Kstar=32$ wells, the finest partition on a validation plateau
($K=5\ldots32$) that three seeds do not resolve, % SRC: w15-crossover-seeds/CROSSOVER-3SEED.md
the store composes held-out pairs at 32.350~$\pm$~0.815 and held-out six-factor corners at
67.177~$\pm$~1.320 distinct valid novel tuples per corner over three seeds, with all four landscape
controls collapsing to exactly 0.0000. % SRC: w12b-nr6-and-kstar/NR6-T7.md
On its 109 held-out corners the store, fitted on 131,072 rows, reads 47.4954 / 47.4037 / 49.1193 % SRC: w12b-nr6-and-kstar/NR6-T7.md; w13-seeds-and-tables/TABLES.md
against 30.0275 / 28.9174 / 37.5138 for the strongest read-path-matched rival of either of our two % SRC: w13-seeds-and-tables/TABLES.md
rival sets, whose 32,256 fit rows carry held-out values the store never saw; on every rung of an % SRC: _thinkpad/w4/spoke-a-rivals/rivals/seed_0/mlp.rival.json
$n$-way ladder it scores above a read-path-matched MLP fitted on the store's own 32,768 fit rows on % SRC: w12-composition-ladder/runs/shipped_seed0/rows_learned_ref_ownpool.json; w12b-nr6-and-kstar/kstar_ladder/2_seed0_K32/LEG.json
every seed, and beats an MLP matched to its whole four-part ledger on those rows by at least
$+3.117$ tuples per corner. % SRC: w12b-nr6-and-kstar/FINDINGS.md
Deletion is store-scoped and exact, and it is not free: the object that forgets is 4.0768 to
4.0897 times the 524,288-byte reference in its four-part ledger across the three seeds, and we
show that on this object the deletion changes what the store holds but not what it answers.
% SRC: w13-referee-2/REFEREE-v1.md; w13-seeds-and-tables/TABLES.md
On a colour maze with an exact integer judge, deletion transfers
(relative residual \sci{7.522}{-16} to \sci{8.826}{-16}, projector residual 0.000), % SRC: w13-seeds-and-tables/TABLES.md
while composition on routes does not transfer through the store: moving the composition verb from
latent space to plan space lifts held-out validity by $+0.4422$ to $+0.5354$, but it lifts a
matched-bytes lookup floor more, so the composition operator belongs to the interface.
% SRC: w14-read-verb/FINAL-REPORT.md
On a transition instrument over the same mazes the store reads 0.7246 on held-out $n$-way
transitions against 0.4035 for the best matched rival over three seeds, with every landscape % SRC: w16-maze-wm/FINDINGS.md
control collapsing; but the store reads a word request with a hand-built pair block that the
rivals, which read the observation, do not receive; when every arm is given the identical input
the margin does not survive, because a matched transformer given the store's own pair design
matches or beats it, so the pair block, not the store, carries it; and no matched arm, the store % SRC: w19-maze-t-symmetric/FINDINGS.md
included, yields a valid chained rollout.
We close with what the naive unit cannot do and with the one measured clue toward a
dynamical successor: the store is exactly the zero-dynamics limit of a settling unit, with

\end{abstract}

\subsection{Positioning: a naive unit, compared with naive units}
\label{sec:positioning}

The Naive CLU is a \emph{naive AI unit}: one model with no system built around it, in the sense
in which an MLP, an LSTM or a transformer is a unit. We compare it only with other naive units, an
MLP, an LSTM, a transformer and a continuous-input MLP, matched on the read path
(\S\ref{sec:object}). We do not claim the best compositional-generation system or the best
unlearning system. Systems built on top of units, such as sharded retraining, compositional
diffusion and physics-informed variants of an MLP, are out of scope by definition; each is cited
in one sentence below, with the unit/system distinction stated.

Generating held-out combinations of factors is an old and active problem~\citep{du2020compositional,liu2022compositional}. The multilinear
line (Tenenbaum and Freeman, 2000\nocite{tenenbaum2000separating}; TensorFaces, Vasilescu and Terzopoulos, 2002\nocite{vasilescu2002tensorfaces}; Vlasic et
al., 2005\nocite{vlasic2005face}; MLC-VAE, 2020~\citep{georgopoulos2020multilinear}) factorises a data tensor organised by every label combination and
renders linearly; the frozen-generator line (GANSpace, 2020~\citep{harkonen2020ganspace}; TCA, 2021~\citep{oldfield2021tensor}; PandA, 2023~\citep{oldfield2023panda}, and its
2024 journal version~\citep{oldfield2024bilinear}) finds editable directions in a pretrained generator without labels and
without an address book. We do not claim either half. In particular we do not claim
multilinear or bilinear factored generation, a request-indexed tensor head, tensor-product
label conditioning, or the downdate primitive, which follows in one line from the summation
form of Cao and Yang (2015)\nocite{cao2015towards} because a covariance is a summation.

Exact unlearning is also established, for $k$-means (Ginart et al., 2019\nocite{ginart2019making}), for bilinear
matrix completion~\citep{xu2023netflix}, and for ridge heads on frozen backbones~\citep{quan2026exact}, where the most recent work
restricts itself explicitly to ridge regression and excludes principal-component,
eigendecomposition and generative models. The nearest generative attempts are approximate~\citep{kong2022approximate}:
feature unlearning for pretrained GANs and VAEs~\citep{moon2024feature} unlearns a feature rather than rows and
fine-tunes the generator; data redaction from pretrained GANs~\citep{kong2023data} states that redaction is a
different task from deletion; GAN unlearning by substitution~\citep{sun2025generative} is approximate by construction.

A systematic screen of both literatures records what we could find. A forward-citation
crawl~\citep{priem2022openalex} over every title and abstract citing the canonical works of either side screened
approximately 4,050 unique works: 3,038 citing the generation-side anchors, 759 citing the
deletion-side anchors, 254 from 21 orthogonal queries that cite neither, and about 100 from
a final targeted sweep. % SRC: _thinkpad/w8/w8-novelty/NOVELTY.md
Deletion language appears in 10 of the 3,038 generation-side works (0.3\%), seven of them
false positives and none exact; no work among the 3,038 combines frozen-decoder,
tensor-head and label-index language (0 of 3,038); the highest number of the six claim
atoms reached by any single work is 4 of 6; and of the 759 deletion-side works none combines
principal-subspace, generative and frozen-model language. % SRC: _thinkpad/w8/w8-novelty/NOVELTY.md
The orthogonal query that asks the question directly, \emph{compositional} $\wedge$
\emph{generative} $\wedge$ \emph{exact deletion}, returns zero works on the screen.
% SRC: _thinkpad/w8/w8-novelty/NOVELTY.md
These are the screen's counts, not a claim that no such model exists: abstract-level screening
cannot see a match buried in a method section, and indexing of preprints younger than about
three months is incomplete (\S\ref{sec:limits}).
Three families of systems built on top of units are out of scope, one sentence each.
SISA training~\citep{bourtoule2021machine} is a system that makes any unit, a generator included,
exactly unlearnable by splitting its data into shards and retraining the unit on the shard that
held the deleted rows, which reads that shard's retained data; we compare with the unit it wraps,
whose only exact route is the retrain of \S\ref{sec:del-comparators}.
Compartmentalized diffusion models (Golatkar, Achille, Swaminathan and Soatto\nocite{golatkar2023training}) are a system that
trains separate diffusion models on separate data sources, composes them at inference and forgets
a source by dropping its model, so what is composed is models of data sources, not held-out
combinations of factor values within one unit.
% cite-tagger: bib entry added by w20 citation-prune -- golatkar2023training (Golatkar, Achille, Swaminathan, Soatto, "Training Data Protection with Compositional Diffusion Models", arXiv:2308.01937)
Physics-informed variants of an MLP, such as physics-informed neural networks (Raissi, Perdikaris
and Karniadakis, 2019\nocite{raissi2019physics}) and Hamiltonian neural networks (Greydanus, Dzamba and Yosinski, 2019\nocite{greydanus2019hamiltonian}), are
systems that add a physical loss or structure to an MLP unit, and the rival we compare with is the
plain MLP unit.
% cite-tagger: bib entries added by w20 citation-prune -- raissi2019physics (Raissi, Perdikaris, Karniadakis, "Physics-informed neural networks", J. Comput. Phys. 378, 2019); greydanus2019hamiltonian (Greydanus, Dzamba, Yosinski, "Hamiltonian Neural Networks", NeurIPS 2019)

One distinction is ours and worth a sentence. The decremental-PCA line
(Hall, Marshall and Martin, 2002\nocite{hall2002adding}, and successors~\citep{jin2014evd,lee2021fast}) subtracts \emph{deflated} eigenspaces; its
authors describe the result as approximating the refit, report that subtraction tends to
instability, and give no rigorous error analysis. Our route is exact for the opposite reason:
\emph{downdate the statistic, not the truncated factors}. The full, untruncated second-moment
statistic is downdated by subtraction and the content is re-eigendecomposed afterwards, so no
truncation error ever enters the subtraction.

\subsection{Why the three properties matter: world models need all three at once}
\label{sec:why}

A world model~\citep{ha2018world,hafner2025mastering} is useful to the extent that it can be asked for states and transitions it
has not seen~\citep{valevski2025diffusion}, and trusted with the answer. We name three properties and use them throughout.
\textbf{Rule validity}: an output is valid if an \emph{exact} judge, integer arithmetic with
no tolerance, accepts it~\citep{vafa2024evaluating}; approximate similarity to a training item does not count.
\textbf{Composition}: the model produces valid outputs for held-out combinations of factor
values that it has seen only separately~\citep{zhao2022toward}. \textbf{Exact deletion}: after a set of training rows
is deleted, the deletable object equals, to floating-point roundoff, the object fitted without
those rows. The three pull against each other: a lookup table deletes exactly but composes
nothing it has not stored; a learned generator composes but can delete exactly only by retraining
(the whole model, or a shard of it~\citep{bourtoule2021machine}); and
approximate unlearning~\citep{guo2020certified,golatkar2020eternal} misses the exact answer in either direction (\S\ref{sec:del-comparators}).
The object we study makes the three properties of one store, and we measure, row by row, where each
holds and where it does not.

\subsection{What we show, and at what thickness}
\label{sec:contrib}

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=2pt]
\item \textbf{Composition at a stated partition, against read-path-matched rivals.} On a
  Shapes3D~\citep{burgess2018shapes,kim2018disentangling} value hold-out at $\Kstar=32$ wells, three scored seeds, with every % SRC: w12b-nr6-and-kstar/NR6-T7.md
  pre-declared landscape control collapsing, the store scores above the strongest read-path-matched
  learned rival of either of the paper's two rival sets on every seed, although that rival's fit rows
  carry held-out values the store never saw (\S\ref{sec:shapes-t7}). On every rung of an $n$-way
  ladder it also scores above a read-path-matched MLP fitted on the store's own 32,768 fit rows, and % SRC: w12b-nr6-and-kstar/kstar_ladder/2_seed0_K32/LEG.json
  beats an MLP matched to its whole ledger on the same rows by at least $+3.117$ per seed % SRC: w12b-nr6-and-kstar/FINDINGS.md
  (\S\ref{sec:ladder}); for $n\ge3$ the rungs are a training-data axis and not a depth axis.
\item \textbf{Exact, store-scoped deletion, with its cost and its bound printed.} Value,
  conjunction and item deletion are light and data-free for the store and cost the rivals a
  full retrain; the four-part byte ledger is printed beside every row; and a probe shows the
  claim's limit: at $\Kstar$ the store forgets the deleted value but the object still answers
  it, because the value identity lives in the address book (\S\ref{sec:shapes-del}).
  Refusal after deletion is a separate, structural mechanism, a hole in a coupling table
  (\S\ref{sec:hole}).
\item \textbf{The same properties on a maze, where deletion transfers through the
  store and composition transfers only on transitions.} Deletion transfers exactly; composition on routes is recovered by a plan-space
  verb, which we report as an \emph{interface} result because it lifts a matched-bytes lookup
  floor more than it lifts the store (\S\ref{sec:maze}). On a transition instrument the store
  carries a composition row with every landscape control collapsing, under a word request with a
  pair block that its rivals do not receive (given the identical input, matched rivals match or
beat it, so that margin belongs to the pair block, not the store); on validation it % SRC: w19-maze-t-symmetric/FINDINGS.md
  stays ahead of rivals trained ten times longer, and its scored replication against such rivals
  keeps the margin while one landscape control misses its collapse bar on one seed; no matched
  arm's chained rollout is valid; and on an observation
  instrument the store gate holds while the margin over rivals is withdrawn (\S\ref{sec:maze-wm}).
\item \textbf{Two ablations and a leaving note.} A composition-only store composes and cannot
  forget; an item store forgets and cannot compose (\S\ref{sec:ablations}). The naive unit's
  limitations, its measured zero-dynamics identity and the theory we carry close the paper
  (\S\ref{sec:limits}--\S\ref{sec:followup}).
\end{enumerate}

\noindent\textbf{Thickness, stated in the introduction.} The positive composition claim rests
on one environment (Shapes3D) at three scored seeds; no row in this study can exceed three
seeds, because seeds 3 and 4 have verified caches but no frozen decoder and no external
classifier, so no corner of theirs can be judged. % SRC: w12b-nr6-and-kstar/FINDINGS.md
On routes, where the maze's exact judge scores whole plans at three scored seeds, the result is
a negative for the store and a positive for the interface. A transition instrument on the same

with a pair block that its rivals do not receive (\S\ref{sec:maze-wm}). Several rows are pending further runs; each is a
marked placeholder whose text names the row and states that it may land as a negative.

\paragraph{The store.} A frozen encoder maps each training item to a latent. The latent
space is cut, without labels, into $K$ wells by a balanced correlation partition; in each well
the store keeps the leading principal subspace of the fit pool, with the pool mean as the
first atom. No label enters the content. We write $n$ for the number of factors jointly
composed by a request and $K$ for the number of wells, and never write both as $k$.

\paragraph{The read.} A request is a tuple of factor values. A tensor-product address book,
fitted on the fit pool, maps the request to per-well coefficients; the read renders
$x_k=U_k c_k$ in each well's orthonormal basis and the frozen decoder renders the image. The
read is request-only: no feature tensor enters it, which is asserted on the deployed object
(bitwise stable under feature perturbation over 64 checks at $\Kstar$).
% SRC: w13-seeds-and-tables/TABLES.md
Labels therefore touch the model only at the address book. This is the interface split:
unsupervised content, a request-indexed head, and a frozen renderer, with no Cartesian-product
training requirement~\citep{wiedemer2023compositional,lachapelle2023additive,montero2022lost}.

\paragraph{The retained statistic and exact deletion.} Beside the content the store keeps, per
well, the running covariance, mean and count of the fit rows. Deleting rows subtracts their
contribution from the statistic; the atoms are re-eigendecomposed from the downdated
statistic; and the address book is carried across the basis change by a closed-form rotation
formed at delete time. No row of data is read. We use one definition of exactness throughout,
and state its scope before any table:

\begin{quote}\small
\textsc{Exact is store-scoped.} After deleting a set of rows, the store content equals, to
float roundoff, the content fitted from the retained rows alone, holding the frozen encoder and
decoder fixed. Neither is refit at any point. It is not retrain-equivalence of the generative
model and it is not a claim that the renderer forgets.
% SRC: w13-seeds-and-tables/TABLES.md
\end{quote}

\noindent The frozen encoder and decoder are declared outside the deletable object. This is
forced, not chosen: the frozen autoencoder's own training set contains evaluation-bank items
(543 / 529 / 552 on the three maze seeds), so the weights fitted with the deleted rows present
are untouched by any deletion. % SRC: w13-seeds-and-tables/TABLES.md
The scoping sentence we carry is the one our deletion theory states for its own store: this is
a store-level guarantee only; the frozen encoder and any residue of past writes in a learned
landscape are separate channels, measured separately; we do not claim certified
$(\varepsilon,\delta)$ unlearning~\citep{guo2020certified}. % SRC: shorts-evidence-inventory.md
Two senses of ``exact'' are kept apart: an exact \emph{judge} is integer arithmetic with no
tolerance; exact \emph{deletion} is the store-scoped definition above.

\paragraph{The four-part ledger, never netted.} Every store row prints four byte counts
separately: (1) the atoms, (2) the covariance and count statistic, (3) the address book, and
(4) the coupling and hole table used for refusal. We never net them into one number, and every
byte figure names its convention (symmetric-triangle float64 for the statistic, unless marked
otherwise). The 524,288-byte figure that recurs below is a \emph{reference} column: the store
of the earlier composition-only design was pinned there, and read-path-matched rivals carry
about 131,072 float32 parameters at it. % SRC: w13-seeds-and-tables/TABLES.md
It is not a budget the deletable object meets.

\paragraph{Rivals: naive against naive.} Learned rivals (MLP, LSTM~\citep{hochreiter1997long}, transformer~\citep{vaswani2017attention}, and a
continuous-input MLP) are matched to the store's \emph{read path}, and fitted from the same
frozen features. At $\Kstar$ the fitted rivals carry 130,433 to 131,891 parameters.
% SRC: w13-seeds-and-tables/TABLES.md
The ledger is memory, not a read-path parameter; it is printed, never matched~\citep{borgeaud2021improving,khandelwal2020generalization,lample2019large}. A lookup table
of written items is a printed \emph{floor}, never a rival. Four controls, which we call the
\emph{pre-declared landscape controls} throughout (\texttt{landscape\_removed}, \texttt{landscape\_shuffled},
\texttt{landscape\_shuffled\_across\_wells}, \texttt{random\_atoms}) must collapse before any
row is claimed for the store; if they do not, the row is an interface row or nothing.

\paragraph{The judge and the estimand.} On Shapes3D an external classifier, hash-pinned per
seed, judges each rendered image; the metric is the number of \emph{distinct valid novel
factor tuples per corner}, a count, with ceilings of 36 (pair corners) and 72 (six-factor
corners). The estimand is paired over corners with a margin of 1.0 tuple per corner declared
before any number. % SRC: w13-seeds-and-tables/TABLES.md
Where an experiment was pre-registered, its success and falsification criteria were written before
its first number; we refer to each as a \emph{pre-registered criterion}, labelled as in that
pre-registration (for example, criterion S1 or criterion S2).
Seed count is a column of every table.

\section{The Naive CLU on Shapes3D: the single design at $\Kstar$}
\label{sec:shapes}

The deletable object is the \emph{single design}: the store, read and ledger of
\S\ref{sec:object} on the Shapes3D value hold-out~\citep{montero2021role,schott2021visual}, with the partition fixed on validation
by a rule frozen before the sweep, $\Kstar=\max\{K : v(5)-\text{mean}_{\text{seeds}}\,v(K)\le
0.02\}$, over $K\in\{5,8,10,12,16,20,24,28,32\}$. Every swept $K$ satisfied the rule, so
$\Kstar=32$, the finest partition swept; the sweep range was not extended to manufacture a
crossover. % SRC: w13-seeds-and-tables/TABLES.md
That is the frozen rule's selection; on three seeds, however, the rule cannot separate the plateau
$K=5\ldots32$: the seed standard deviation of validation composition exceeds the rule's 0.02
tolerance at $K=10$, 16, 20, 24 and 28 (0.0211, 0.0203, 0.0285, 0.0235, 0.0276) and at $\Kstar=32$ % SRC: w15-crossover-seeds/CROSSOVER-3SEED.md
(0.0259), so three seeds do not distinguish $\Kstar$ from its neighbours. % SRC: w15-crossover-seeds/CROSSOVER-3SEED.md
The finding is the plateau itself: composition is flat across $K$ to within its seed spread, while
the honest four-part ledger falls to its minimum near $K=24$, 2,094,130 / 2,080,090 / 2,085,810 B % SRC: w15-crossover-seeds/CROSSOVER-3SEED.md
on seeds 0 / 1 / 2, a minimum that is resolvable (seed band 7,060 B against neighbour gaps of % SRC: w15-crossover-seeds/CROSSOVER-3SEED.md
12,203 B and 30,507 B; Appendix~\ref{app:kcurve}, Table~\ref{tab:kcurve3}). % SRC: w15-crossover-seeds/CROSSOVER-3SEED.md
The main text carries this one $K$. The $K=5$ endpoint of the sweep is reported in
Appendix~\ref{app:k5}, labelled as the endpoint, with its sign stated as a property of $K=5$.

\subsection{Exact deletion on the single design: value, conjunction, item}
\label{sec:shapes-del}

Three deletions are run on the $\Kstar$ object on TEST. \emph{Value}: every fit row carrying
red. \emph{Conjunction}: every fit row carrying red \emph{and} sphere; we call it the red
sphere everywhere. The conjunction first named in the design, red $\wedge$ cube, deletes zero rows,
because cube is the shape held out of the split entirely; it is printed as vacuous and never as
a deletion result. \emph{Item}: a random 1\% of fit rows. Table~\ref{tab:del} gives each with
its cost. \emph{Forgetting a value $c$} is three operations on one object: downdate the store,
downdate the address book's statistics, and punch the hole for $c$ (\S\ref{sec:hole}). Every
deletion row therefore prints four measurements: (a) the store equals the store refit without the
rows, a result; (b) the address book's statistics equal those refit without the rows, a result;
(c) requests for $c$ are refused through the hole, a display row only, because the hole is a flag
and symbolic refusal is not claimed; and (d) composition on the other values is unchanged, a
result. Post-deletion adaptation of the store is \emph{light and data-free}: the covariance
and count are downdated by subtraction and the atoms are re-eigendecomposed from the downdated
statistic, with no row of data read. The (a) and (d) rows printed here were measured with the
address book carried across the basis change, not downdated; the address-book downdate is new,
and its cells are named placeholders in Table~\ref{tab:del}.
The reference can delete only by refitting from scratch without the deleted rows.

\subsection{What the rivals can do instead: retrain without, or approximate}
\label{sec:del-comparators}

A learned rival's only exact deletion route is to retrain from scratch without the rows. We
built that route for every rival family, at matched compute, and two approximate unlearners
beside it, measured on the fit pool (16,384 rows; no held-out corner and no scored run).
Retraining without the red rows costs each rival 8.86 to 34.62 s and reads 15,032 data rows
(Table~\ref{tab:rivals-del}); the approximate unlearners are appendix rows
(Appendix~\ref{app:delbase}) because they are not scored rows of this study, and because
they miss the exact answer. % SRC: w12-deletion-baselines/FINDINGS.md

\noindent Against that exact reference, NegGrad+ over-forgets by 9.4 to 20.8 times and
fine-tuning on the retained rows under-forgets on three of four arms, though both were given
every gradient row-visit the exact retrain got (296,960, asserted equal across all eight arm
and method combinations on all three seeds). % SRC: w12-deletion-baselines/FINDINGS.md
The conjunction row confirms, on the rivals themselves, that composition survives a
conjunction deletion: retraining a learned rival without the red sphere rows leaves red-sphere
reconstruction essentially unchanged (3-seed means $-0.00289$, $+0.01907$, $-0.00179$,
$-0.00026$), because red and sphere each survive separately and the rival composes them.
% SRC: w12-deletion-baselines/FINDINGS.md
That is composition working, not deletion failing, and it is a property of compositional
generation rather than of our store. It is also why the conjunction row cannot carry a
refusal claim; the hole does.

\subsection{Refusal after deletion: the hole, a separate mechanism}
\label{sec:hole}

Punching a hole for a deleted value or conjunction marks the request slot as refused. On the
$\Kstar$ object two holes cost 2 bytes, read 0 data rows, take 0.0000 s and refit nothing, in
a 1,322-byte coupling table whose layout is asserted equal to the field store's over 1,322 pair
slots. % SRC: w13-seeds-and-tables/TABLES.md
One hole (red $\wedge$ cube) is refusable although no red cube was ever written: refusal does
not need a deleted row to have existed. % SRC: w13-seeds-and-tables/TABLES.md

holed value is refused by construction, so refusal is measurement (c) of Table~\ref{tab:del}, a
display row, and symbolic refusal is not claimed. An item deletion makes nothing impossible: 0 of 164
requests built from the deleted rows are certifiably impossible, on all three seeds, so the
item row has no refusal row at all. % SRC: w12-deletion-baselines/FINDINGS.md

\subsection{Fixing the request layer (CMAZE-2b)}
\label{sec:req-layer}

On CMAZE-2 the blind-sequential planner, which ignores key order, was valid on 0.9077 /
0.8905 / 0.9128 of held-out unseen-pair routes, against the published 0.4980 of CMAZE-1: 1,242
of 1,449 optimal routes picked up exactly one key, so there was no key order for the second rule
to trap. % SRC: w13-seeds-and-tables/TABLES.md
The cause was the layer's own acceptance test. A maze whose door cut needs two keys cannot
supply the reverse direction at all, because the key that opens the cut door lives on one side
of the cut, and the layer required pairs in both directions; the symmetry requirement selected
single-key cuts. % SRC: w14-request-layer-fix/FINAL-REPORT.md
CMAZE-2b adds one integer rule, keep a pair iff its optimal route takes at least two keys, and
changes nothing else: the fraction of routes needing at least two keys goes from 0.1428 to
1.0000, blind-sequential on held-out unseen pairs falls from 0.9077 to 0.1818--0.2344, and the
oracle stays at 1.0000 (Tables~\ref{tab:req} and~\ref{tab:req2b}, Appendix~\ref{app:verb}). % SRC: w14-request-layer-fix/FINAL-REPORT.md

The fix lands in CMAZE-1's own multi-key band rather than at its mixture value of 0.4980, and % SRC: w14-request-layer-fix/FINAL-REPORT.md
its costs are declared (Appendix~\ref{app:verb}).

\subsection{The clue: the store is the zero-dynamics limit of a settling unit}
\label{sec:clue}

The store we evaluate is the zero-dynamics limit of the CLU: the same content, the same wells,
the same read, with the settling loop's step count set to zero. Inside a well this is an
algebraic identity: the store's energy is the distance to the atom span, the request's basin sits
at an address inside that span, so both minima coincide and an in-well settle has nowhere to go
(derivation in Appendix~\ref{app:clue}). We asserted this in code: the zero-step arm was required to be bit-identical to
the static read by \texttt{np.array\_equal} over 256 requests, and it was, on all three scored
seeds, returning the static read's held-out scores to every printed digit (row 4: 63.612 /
64.327 / 67.653; row 3: 29.717 / 31.617 / 31.450). % SRC: w12-clue-theorist/CLUE.md
The only place dynamics can act is across wells: with a bilinear low-rank coupling $\lambda W$
the settled state is $z^\star=(\beta I-\lambda W)^{-1}\beta z_{\text{req}}$, which returns
$z_{\text{req}}$ exactly at $\lambda=0$. Switching the dynamics on costs composition at every
strength at which they move the state: $-0.531$ / $-0.510$ / $-0.388$ six-factor tuples per
corner at the gentlest setting that moves it (median displacement 0.009), and $-39.959$ /
$-46.918$ / $-39.061$ at full strength (median displacement 0.39), while paying 81,312 to 84,216
bytes of read-side coupling the static read does not. % SRC: w12-clue-theorist/CLUE.md
The identity holds under four named conditions (Appendix~\ref{app:clue}).

\subsection{The theory we carry: the Latent Dynamics Model class}
\label{sec:theory}

We call the class of objects that this store is the static corner of a \emph{Latent Dynamics
Model}: a store whose content is read by relaxing a latent state under a designed energy~\citep{hopfield1982neural,krotov2016dense,ramsauer2021hopfield,hoover2023energy,kong2024reservoir}, of
which the Naive CLU is the member with the relaxation switched off. (The phrase is used in
reduced-order modelling and in model-based reinforcement learning for a learned surrogate of an
environment's dynamics; here the dynamics are the read, not a model of the data.) Its laws are this paper's own theory, each with a status label and rider (further laws in Appendix~\ref{app:shorts}).

\paragraph{Two laws measured on this study's own store (\textsc{confirmed [this study]}).}
Switched on, the field store's relaxation obeys a curvature--retention relationship whose
prediction is a \emph{time}, not a score: with nothing fitted, it predicts the settle half-life to
a median factor of 1.105 on 49 held-out corners and three seeds (bar 0.10 on the log error; 0.0433
/ 0.0435 / 0.0431), and beats a one-parameter form fitted with sight of the held-out measurements
on 49 of 49 corners on every seed; one of six pre-registered criteria (the V-curve argmin) misses on
all three seeds, and the law predicts where its simplest form stops.
% SRC: w10-physics-section-evidence/EVIDENCE.md
Deletion commutes with scheduled decay on a learned store, 0 bytes differing in a 2,054,866-byte
image, while the stronger claim that the post-delete store equals the never-inserted one is
\textsc{refuted} (65.17\% to 65.39\% of the image differs, 83.5\% of it because the address book
and coupling table are fitted on the pool). % SRC: w10-physics-section-evidence/EVIDENCE.md
\emph{These are dynamics results about our own store. They occupy no row of the evaluation, they
beat no model, and we make no capability claim from them}; no arm of ours fails the retention law,
so it distinguishes nothing. The full statements, criteria and misses are in
Appendix~\ref{app:physics}.

\subsection{Leaving note}
\label{sec:leaving}

The naive unit buys exact deletion by subtraction and, at a stated partition, composition on
Shapes3D that scores above the strongest read-path-matched learner of either of our rival sets
and above a read-path-matched MLP fitted on the store's own rows. It does not buy composition on an exact-judge route task, where the operator is
task-shaped and belongs to the interface; it does not stop the object answering a deleted value;
and it pays for exactness in bytes. Switched on, the dynamics cost composition at every strength
that moves the state, while a law that no arm of ours fails predicts their relaxation: so far a
\emph{law about the store}, not a \emph{lever on the task}. The experiment that would make the law a
claim about a class, a comparator predicted in advance to be mispredicted in the
$h/h^\star\in[0.5,2]$ bucket, is named and not run. % SRC: w10-physics-section-evidence/EVIDENCE.md

\section{Reporting conventions}
\label{app:conventions}

\paragraph{Provenance.} Every number in the source of this paper carries a comment naming the
artefact it was read from. Numbers are quoted as the artefact prints them; none is re-derived.
Where two artefacts print the same quantity at different precision, the one cited is the one the
number was taken from.

\paragraph{Two byte conventions, and which object carries which.} The statistic of every object
in the main text is priced on the symmetric-triangle float64 convention,
$\sum_k d_k(d_k+1)/2\times8$ B plus means and counts; the $\Kstar$ object's covariance is
549,120 B on it, which closes to the byte. % SRC: w13-seeds-and-tables/TABLES.md; w13-referee-2/REFEREE-v1.md
One object is priced on the full-square convention, $8\sum_k d_k^2$: the $K=5$ store of the
ladder, on the label-derived partition. We name the two $K=5$ objects apart everywhere:
\emph{$K=5$ (MI partition, full square)}, the ladder's store, well widths 777 / 936 / 216 / 60 /
59 on seed 0, statistic 12,284,920 B; and \emph{$K=5$ (ward, triangle)}, the crossover and
bridge store, five wells of width 409--410, statistic 3,391,552 B. They differ in partition and
in convention; the convention alone is a factor of about two.
% SRC: w13-referee-2/REFEREE-v1.md; w12b-nr6-and-kstar/FINDINGS.md; w13-seeds-and-tables/TABLES.md
The ladder's ledger file carries three parts; its coupling table is a declared absence, not a
zero. % SRC: w13-seeds-and-tables/TABLES.md

\paragraph{Seeds and configurations.} Seed count and configuration count are separate fields. The
phrase ``nine seeds'' never applies: where nine legs appear they are three configurations times
three seeds inside one implementation (Appendix~\ref{app:impl}).

\section{The zero-dynamics identity: conditions and provenance}
\label{app:clue}

\paragraph{The derivation.} The content is an orthonormal basis $U_k$ of the
per-well atom span, and the read renders the request's address through that basis,
$x_k=U_k c_k$. The store's energy for a basis is the distance to its span,
$E_{\text{store},k}=\tfrac12\operatorname{dist}^2(x_k,\operatorname{span}G_k)$, zero everywhere
on the span; the request enters as a basin at the address, $E_{\text{req},k}=\tfrac12\beta\lVert
x_k-a_k\rVert^2$, zero at the address. Because $a_k\in\operatorname{span}(G_k)$ by construction
of the renderer, both terms attain their minima at the same point, so an in-well settle has
nowhere to go. 

\paragraph{The four conditions.} (C1) The store's energy is distance to the atom span, the atoms
being a basis: with an atoms-as-points energy on the same content the settle moves (relative
displacement median 1.7244, max 2.6682 on a synthetic check). (C2) The address lies in the span:
pushed off the span by $\delta=0.10$ the settle moves by exactly $\delta/(1+\beta)$, measured
0.050000 against the closed form 0.050000. (C3) The state is carried in span coordinates; the same
energy in channel coordinates drifts by \sci{7.447}{-08} relative, float32 rounding, so the paper
says ``identical'' of the channel form and ``bit-identical'' only of the span form. (C4) No
cross-well term: at $\lambda=0$ the displacement is exactly 0.0; at
$\lambda=10^{-4}$, $10^{-2}$, $0.05$, $0.2$ it is \sci{7.875}{-05}, \sci{7.882}{-03},
\sci{3.971}{-02}, \sci{1.720}{-01}. Two wells of the deployed store were full rank, where an in-well
settle is trivially a no-op. The coercivity certificate reproduces from first principles:
$\lambda^\star=0.25000000$ and a certificate minimum eigenvalue of $-1.50000000$ at the declared
$\lambda=1$, so the landscape is unbounded below at the declared strength and the used strength is
0.225000. % SRC: w12-clue-theorist/CLUE.md
The identity is in-well only; a $\lambda=0$, steps$>0$ arm was never run on the deployed store (the
synthetic check covers steps 0 / 1 / 10 / 1000); and the \sci{1.9}{-7} off-span residual is the
renderer's float32 rounding, the same size for the static store that never moves.
% SRC: w12-clue-theorist/CLUE.md

\paragraph{Provenance of the retention law.} Re-derived from the raw result files, the law's
headline reproduces: 0.043256 / 0.043516 / 0.043102 and $-0.596831$ / $-0.579639$ / $-0.598172$,
49 of 49 on every seed; the integrator is the theory's map to \sci{1.884682}{-08}; the byte pin is

\section{The prior-derivation ledger}
\label{app:shorts}

The laws below
are this paper's own theory. Each carries a status: \textsc{confirmed [this study]} where it was
measured on this study's own store; \textsc{confirmed [prior derivation]} where it was derived and
validated on designed or small trained testbeds outside this study's store and was not re-run
here; \textsc{refuted} and \textsc{untested} as stated. Status discipline is part of the section.


\paragraph{Store-level exact deletion in a designed store (\textsc{confirmed [prior
derivation]}).} With an order-independent waitlist, store-level deletion is exact at every load
measured, 0.29 to 1.71 times lattice capacity, a membership signal of AUC 0.5000 $\pm$ 0.0000 on all
six statistics and byte equality 1.0000, under three stated conditions (budget at least the number
of cells, zero leak, priority- or attribute-based eviction; recency-based eviction is excluded as
intrinsically history-dependent). % SRC: shorts-evidence-inventory.md
The placement rule is the Blelloch--Golovin stable-matching table~\citep{blelloch2007strongly} with a global priority order;
the theorems follow from theirs and the fix-up cascade is theirs. Its scoping is the one this
paper adopts in \S\ref{sec:object}: a store-level guarantee only, the frozen encoder and any
residue of past writes in a learned landscape being separate channels, and no certified
$(\varepsilon,\delta)$ unlearning claimed. % SRC: shorts-evidence-inventory.md

\paragraph{The damping optimum (\textsc{confirmed [prior derivation]}; \textsc{refuted} as a
rollout prediction on this store).} On the exact one-step Jacobian the emergent retention
half-life is a V-curve in the damping, with its argmin at $0.902\pm0.003$ of
$\gamma_{\text{crit}}$ and slopes $-1.0020$ and $+1.116$ on three of three emergent seeds, one
damping-optimum curve at two values of the curvature rather than two laws.
% SRC: shorts-evidence-inventory.md
It holds only with fluctuation--dissipation-consistent noise and a Newtonian kinetic term. On
this study's field store the rollout misses the same argmin on all three seeds (above), with the
failing step named: the pure-exponential envelope is invalid at the defective point.
% SRC: w10-physics-section-evidence/EVIDENCE.md

\paragraph{Interference is degree-bounded (\textsc{confirmed [prior derivation]}).} Cross-unit
basin displacement is exactly zero off the coupling graph (0 of 4656 off-graph entries nonzero
across 72 runs, three topologies), so interference is the degree times the per-edge displacement,
flat in the number of units at fixed degree, while a shared-potential monolith grows as
$N^{1.18\pm0.17}$. The firewall is parameter separation, not block structure.
% SRC: shorts-evidence-inventory.md

\paragraph{Asserted, not derived (\textsc{untested}).} Eight further statements of the theory,
including a lifetime trilemma whose consequence is measured, have no derivation and no
impossibility argument, and are carried as untested; a certificate family for reach and escape is
untested on this store, with its own measured limit that unit Jacobian determinant and zero
energy change are not sufficient for bounded dynamics (a certified excursion of 0.09 against
104.83 blind). % SRC: w13-draft-revise/DRAFT-v1.md; w10-physics-section-evidence/EVIDENCE.md
A law this study planned against, that a Mexican-hat width or separation sets the
interpolation floor and the refusal gap, does not exist in its own derivations, and the nearest
derived object inverts it: the retention floor is curvature-independent. % SRC: w10-physics-section-evidence/EVIDENCE.md


These results were derived and validated on designed or small trained testbeds before this study
and were not re-run on this study's store; their content enters here as this paper's own theory,
each with the rider that must travel with it. % SRC: shorts-evidence-inventory.md
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=1pt]
\item \textbf{Retention triad (qualitative).} A latch with infinite lifetime, a $\mu^{-2}$ budget
  law and bounded drift are qualitatively absent in learned recurrent baselines (median lifetime 5.6
  / 56 / 69 map-steps for three recurrent families; the emergent unit 263 steps, bounded 0.35 rad).
  Rider: a per-step compute factor of 6.2 against an LSTM inverts retention per unit of compute, so
  no ``longer'' claim is made on compute. % SRC: shorts-evidence-inventory.md
\item \textbf{GMOR~\citep{gellmann1968behavior} on trained checkpoints.} With a linear ambient spurion the three objects satisfy
  $\mu^2F^2=\delta\Sigma$ to a maximum absolute deviation of \sci{1.33}{-15} on 8 trained SO(2)
  checkpoints. Rider: designed SO(2), probe-only, float64. % SRC: shorts-evidence-inventory.md
\item \textbf{Erosion law and anchor cure.} Contrastive wake--sleep training inverts a designed
  vacuum if and only if the flat direction is unconstrained by the wake phase; an anchor cures it,
  and every headline law survives past the erosion horizon under the cure. % SRC: shorts-evidence-inventory.md
\item \textbf{Friction preserves, temperature erases.} The coset diffusion law is verified to
  $1.0068\pm0.0219$ over 25 cells; damping 0.05 to 0.2 lengthens coset half-life
  $3.77\pm0.23$ times. Rider: two observables, two signs; a figure that does not name the initial
  condition is meaningless. % SRC: shorts-evidence-inventory.md
\item \textbf{The friction-hole vault.} $107.77\pm4.78$ times (the $\hat D$-estimator number; raw
  first-passage $86.97\pm2.94$), against a scalar friction of the same strength at
  $13.28\pm0.12$. % SRC: shorts-evidence-inventory.md
\item \textbf{Priced channel.} Sync time within 7.5\% of a registered prediction; the half-life
  claim is ranking-only; the price law holds at $N\in\{2,4,8,16\}$. % SRC: shorts-evidence-inventory.md
\item \textbf{Reversible O(1)-memory training.} At zero damping, recompute-backwards training
  matches stored-activation gradients to \sci{2.1}{-6} relative in float32, with peak activation
  memory 6.3 KB against 6.00 MB at $T=1024$; exact only at zero damping. % SRC: shorts-evidence-inventory.md
\item \textbf{Hard read isolation (designed store only).} A sub-barrier particle cannot read a
  foreign item; the predicted threshold mass 0.36 is bracketed by the sweep (crosses at 0.34,
  confines at 0.38). Rider: it does not transfer to a trained block. % SRC: shorts-evidence-inventory.md
\item \textbf{The trilemma.} With a quadratic payload channel, an anti-decoration guard and a fixed
  read budget, a store cannot have all three of exact value fidelity, amplitude-independent address
  hold and amplitude-independent read latency; both proposed fixes are refuted.
  % SRC: shorts-evidence-inventory.md
\end{itemize}
Binding scope for every item: these laws govern the store; end-to-end performance additionally
depends on the encoder, measured separately. % SRC: shorts-evidence-inventory.md

\section{The encoder: adaptation and the unfreezing decomposition}
\label{app:encoder-main}

\subsection*{Light encoder adaptation (secondary)}

The frozen encoder is the primary object: a harness on top of a large encoder that is never
unfrozen. Light adaptation is optional, and this is what it buys. At $\Kstar$, three seeds,
equal steps and images across arms (400 steps, 25,600 images; encoder parameters in the
optimiser 0 / 131,072 / 428,576), the margin of the single design over the best learned
baseline grows from the frozen encoder under the \emph{anchor} arm (full unfreeze with a
penalty on distance from the frozen weights) on all three seeds: $+0.5688\to+1.3303$,
$-0.0275\to-0.0092$, $+1.1009\to+1.3028$. The low-rank \emph{adapter} grows it on two of
three ($+1.6239$, $+0.3670$, and $+0.3028$, a loss of $0.7982$ on seed 2).
% SRC: w13-encoder-adapt/CROSS-ARM.md
The comparator here is a 180k-parameter MLP, a different opponent from Table~\ref{tab:t7}, so
the two tables are never merged; a factor probe measured as a margin over random
initialisation buys nothing ($+0.0001$ to $-0.0160$ aggregate, per seed, never pooled).
% SRC: w13-encoder-adapt/CROSS-ARM.md; w13-advisor-report.md

\subsection*{Unfreezing with the store on the gradient path: the decomposition}

An earlier CPU leg (shortened decoder, 400 joint steps, 8,192 training images, 3 seeds; a
different instrument, reported in its own table, Appendix~\ref{app:encloop}) asked whether
training the encoder through the store improves composition. The net is a wash because two
real effects cancel: unfreezing the encoder with the store on the gradient path hurts ($A'$
negative on 5 of 6 seed-rows) and a swap loss repairs that damage ($A''$ positive on 4 of 6;
the four two-sided sign-test $p$-values are 0.0001751, 0.000198, 0.000346 and 0.0001564, three
of them at or below 0.0003). This is a finding about the encoder, and the claim
that the store shapes the representation is not made. % SRC: w13-seeds-and-tables/TABLES.md; w13-draft-revise/DRAFT-v1.md
A longer leg at ten times the steps (4,000 joint steps, 256,000 images per arm) corroborates
the direction of the swap-loss ingredient on its probe margin, $A''$ positive on all three
seeds ($+0.0100$ / $+0.0129$ / $+0.0075$), while its equal-seconds control is short by 41.2 /
27.8 / 18.6 s and is biased toward the treatment. % SRC: w8-BAR-D-fold.md
\paragraph{The decomposition re-stated on the longer leg.} On the
longer, full-decoder leg (Table~\ref{tab:bard-gpu}, a block of its own, never merged with the
shortened-decoder leg of Table~\ref{tab:encloop}) the ordering is \textbf{not reproduced} by its
pre-declared rule, which asks for $A'<0$ and $A''>0$ each in at least four of six cells: $A'$ is
negative in only 2 of 6 cells, while $A''$ is positive in 6 of 6 (its interval excludes 0 in 5 of % SRC: w15-bar-d-score/FINDINGS.md
6). The swap-loss half survives and strengthens (positive in 4 of 6 cells on the shortened leg, 6 % SRC: w15-bar-d-score/FINDINGS.md
of 6 on the longer one); the ``unfreezing hurts'' half does not survive. Unfreezing \emph{helps} by
about +32 tuples per corner on row 4 at seeds 0 and 2 and hurts only at seed 1, the one seed whose % SRC: w15-bar-d-score/FINDINGS.md
\texttt{alone} control trained well (probe margin over random initialisation $-0.0505$ / % SRC: w15-bar-d-score/FINDINGS.md
$+0.0242$ / $-0.0862$; row-4 score 38.5 / 64.4 / 39.3). ``Unfreezing hurts'' is therefore not a % SRC: w15-bar-d-score/FINDINGS.md
general finding: at these pins it is a property of one seed's well-trained control. What both legs
support is that the swap loss helps.

\section{The two laws on this study's store, stated in full}
\label{app:laws}

\paragraph{Retention on the store (\textsc{confirmed [this study]}).} Switched on, the field
store's relaxation obeys a curvature--retention relationship (CRR) whose prediction is a
\emph{time}, not a score. On the damped velocity-Verlet map the store deploys, the slow
multiplier gives $n_{1/2}=\ln2/(-\ln\lvert\lambda_{\text{slow}}\rvert)$ with nothing fitted. On 49
held-out corners and three seeds the median $\lvert\log_{10}(n_{\text{meas}}/n_{\text{pred}})\rvert$ is
0.0433 / 0.0435 / 0.0431 against a pre-registered bar of 0.10, a factor of 1.105 at the median
corner, seed spread 0.0004; and it beats a one-parameter functional form \emph{fitted with sight
of the held-out measurements} on 49 of 49 corners on every seed (mean paired $\Delta$ $-0.5968$ /
$-0.5796$ / $-0.5982$; that fitted form's own median error is 0.640132 / 0.623155 / 0.641281).
% SRC: w10-physics-section-evidence/EVIDENCE.md
On an exact quadratic, where the law holds by construction, the same measurement chain recovers it
only to a median of 0.0349 (p90 0.0749, max 0.1601 over 17 cells), so the 0.10 bar is about three
times the instrument's floor. % SRC: w10-physics-section-evidence/EVIDENCE.md
The law predicts where its own simplest form stops: the median error is 0.0008 at $h/h^\star<0.1$
and 0.1482 in the one bucket straddling the exceptional point, where the $2\times2$ map is
defective. % SRC: w10-physics-section-evidence/EVIDENCE.md
One of the six pre-registered criteria missed on all three seeds: the V-curve argmin sits at 0.3362 /
0.3301 / 0.3520 of $\gamma_{\text{crit}}$ against a factor-2 bar, a miss pre-registered with its
cause before the run; and the stricter reading of a second bar, its maximum over $\gamma$, is
printed and not moved: 59.1158 / 0.1612 / 1883.2934 against 0.10.
% SRC: w10-physics-section-evidence/EVIDENCE.md
\emph{This is a dynamics result about our own store. It occupies no row of the evaluation, it
beats no model, and we make no capability claim from it}; no arm of ours fails the law, so the law
distinguishes nothing, which is why it is theory and not a result row.

\paragraph{Deletion commutes with decay (\textsc{confirmed [this study]}) and its companion
(\textsc{refuted}).} On a learned store with scheduled per-item decay,
$\text{evict}\circ\text{decay}^n=\text{decay}^n\circ\text{evict}$ with 0 bytes differing in a
2,054,866-byte image, 12 of 12 cells per seed, three seeds, zero tolerance.
% SRC: w10-physics-section-evidence/EVIDENCE.md
Its companion, that the post-delete store is byte-identical to the store built from the
never-inserted history, fails as pre-registered: 1,339,083 / 1,338,974 / 1,343,641 bytes differ
(65.17\% / 65.16\% / 65.39\% of the image), and the break-out shows that placement is only 16.5\%
of the obstruction and 83.5\% is that the address book and coupling table are fitted on the pool.
% SRC: w10-physics-section-evidence/EVIDENCE.md
That falsifies the precondition (canonical Blelloch--Golovin placement) on this store, not the
law. Two consequences derived before measurement were then exact: the refusal horizon is $19.9316$
half-lives for every curvature, measured at 539 steps $=19.9432$ half-lives on all three seeds.
% SRC: w10-physics-section-evidence/EVIDENCE.md
