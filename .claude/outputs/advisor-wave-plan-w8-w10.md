## 0. Binding rules (in addition to the w4–w7 plan's standing rules)
- **Same encoder for every arm.** In the shapes cell: every arm maps the request to the frozen feature space and renders through the shared frozen decoder and judge. In the maze: one shared encoder, every arm consumes its features, one shared action head, one exact judge. When the encoder is unfrozen, each arm trains its own encoder at EQUAL compute.
- **No run-killing tripwires.** Instrument checks (fragment-lookup score, composition-blind score, table floor) are REPORTED COLUMNS, never abort conditions. Every commissioned experiment runs the CLU and the learned baselines to completion.
- **Row classes:** RESULT rows are scored vs learned baselines (MLP, LSTM, transformer, continuous MLP) at matched parameters; the prototype table is a printed floor. DISPLAY rows (post-deletion refusal, post-deletion composition, refusal of impossible requests) show inductive bias; the only comparator there is a transformer with a refuse token trained on impossible examples, and our line is "no impossible example ever seen".
- Scored budget: new targets `single-design`, `encoder-loop`, `maze` get 3 each; `generation-table` has 1 left; replications free; 3 seeds on every number.
- **Paper object (Head):** the composition-led store = the **Naive-CLU**. Physics = an extended section (theory + the shorts' validations + w6 CRR), benchmark for the physics CLU declared future work. Outlook = latent-dynamics clue, explicitly unsupported.
- Novelty: if the scout finds prior art that IS our object (multilinear/bilinear factored generation with a request-indexed tensor head), the Hub STOPS on that claim and flags it to the Head. Exact deletion on the same object is the differentiator to check next.
