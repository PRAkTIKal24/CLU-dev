## 0. Binding rules (in addition to the w4–w7 and w8–w10 standing rules)
- **No kill conditions in any task.** Every commissioned arm runs to completion. The Hub may abort only for absolute wasted compute and must log the reasoning before killing. Instrument checks are printed columns.
- **The single design is 1×.** The composition-only subspace store (no ledger) is the cheaper special case (~1/7.5×) and is printed as an ABLATION row ("composes, cannot forget"). The wave-4 item store is the other ablation ("forgets, loses composition"). The four-part ledger (atoms · covariance/count ledger · address book · coupling/hole table) is printed on every row, never netted. Learned baselines are matched to the single design's TOTAL bytes (1×); the old 524,288 B matching is printed as a reference column.
- **Deletion language (Head):** never "no changes after deletion". Say: post-deletion adaptation is LIGHT and DATA-FREE (re-eigendecompose the ledger + carry the address book; no row of data read), versus learned baselines that can only delete by retraining from scratch without the deleted rows. Print the adaptation cost (seconds, bytes rewritten, data rows read) beside every deletion row for every arm.
- **Two exact mechanisms on one object, stated as such:** (i) data forgetting = covariance/count DOWNDATE (item, value, or conjunction rows); (ii) refusal after deletion = the request count/coupling table with HOLES. A compositional store can still compose "red cube" from "red" and "cube" after the red-cube rows are gone — that is composition working, not deletion failing — so the refusal row is what the hole is for. Both are reported, separately, on the same object.
- **Frozen encoder is the paper's primary narrative** (a harness on top of a large, expensive world-model encoder that is never unfrozen). Encoder adaptation (wave 13) is a secondary line; every baseline receives the same adapted features.
- Seeds: 3 minimum everywhere; **5 seeds on the composition ladder and the headline deletion rows** if generation-2 caches for seeds 3–4 exist in time (spoke below). Declared 3-seed rows are never mixed with 5-seed rows in one plot without a printed n.
- Novelty wording: never "multilinear/bilinear factored generation with a tensor head". Claim ladder = (1) exact deletion on a compositional generative store, (2) the interface split (unsupervised content from a frozen encoder's latent + request-indexed head + frozen decoder ⇒ no Cartesian-product training requirement), (3) wells as a use. "Downdate the statistic, not the truncated factors" is the deletion sentence.
