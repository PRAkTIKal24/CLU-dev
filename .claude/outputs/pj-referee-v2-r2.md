## 2. Itemized findings

### MUST-FIX (block submission)

**MF-1 — Two different anchored retention slopes for the same run, neither identified.** Location: §4.4 line 268 ("the slope holds at −0.956") vs Fig. 3 legend ("slope −0.961 (pred −1)") and caption ("fitted −0.961 against predicted −1"). Attack: this is precisely the §3.4↔App-D class of internal contradiction the register's M4 exists to prevent; a reviewer reproducing across sections finds two slopes for one dataset and stops trusting the 12-digit claims. Evidence: `figure-render-pass.md:101,207` establishes both are *correct but different statistics* on the same npz (−0.96062 = seed-mean OLS over the 7 overdamped δ; −0.956 = per-point fit over all overdamped rows) and **explicitly warned**: "If anything downstream cites 'the overdamped retention slope', it should say which fit." The draft cites both and says neither. Fix: quote one number in both places, or add the fit-spec clause to each.

**MF-2 — "This chapter" in a workshop submission.** Location: §1 Contributions, line 65 / tex line 44: *"This chapter provides a summary of the transverse-curvature price list…"*. Attack: instant credibility hit, and worse — it signals the paper is extracted from a larger internal document, feeding exactly the M2 "reviewer reviews the program" de-anonymization surface. Fix: "This paper".

**MF-3 — Citation of an unread, unverified, authorless poster.** Location: §2 Delineation, lines 106–108 ("recent efforts utilize symmetry regularization to induce flat directions (NeurReps 2025 workshop poster)") + the authorless reference entry "Symmetry-regularized learning of continuous attractor dynamics (2025). NeurReps 2025 workshop poster." Attack: the scout that surfaced this item marked it **F2: "authors, exact title casing and abstract UNVERIFIED… Do not cite until read"** (`audience-refresh-2025-2026.md` §1.5 N2 + Confidence & gaps). An authorless entry in a hand-built list invites "did you actually read this?" — and the honest answer is no. Fix: obtain and read the OpenReview PDF (`W8Gf7CYCo8`), or soften to uncited prose ("concurrent workshop work has explored soft symmetry regularization for continuous attractors") and drop the reference entry.

**MF-4 — corr = 0.9987 quoted without its "overdamped-only" scope.** Location: §4.2 line 226: "(corr(log pred, log meas) = 0.9987)". Attack: silent scope-widening (M4 class). Evidence: `v2-full-runs.md:81` — "corr(log pred, log meas): **0.9987 overdamped-only** (his regime…), **0.973 pooled** (the drop *is* the regime structure)." As printed, a reader takes 0.9987 as the pooled correlation — which would *contradict* the very failure the sentence asserts. The pooled 0.973 actually strengthens the paper's own point. Fix: "0.9987 overdamped-only; 0.973 pooled — the drop is the regime structure."

**MF-5 — The Mo claim boundary is internally inconsistent (both directions, in one paper).** Locations: Abstract lines 6–8 ("the quantitative exchange rate … **remains unmeasured**"); §2 line 116 ("We do not claim novelty over these **qualitative** lifetime predictions"); §4.2 line 224 ("closely matching **their reported median of 1.013**"). Attack: the hostile reviewer holds these three sentences side by side: you call the prior work qualitative and the quantity unmeasured, then calibrate against that same prior work's *published quantitative median*. Evidence: `mo-deep-read.md:120,160` fixes the defensible form — Mo's account is *kinematic* (measured pseudo-gap → lifetime, estimator-based, median 1.013); what is genuinely unmeasured before this paper is the *constitutive* map (potential curvature → gap → two-branch law + floor + EP), which a first-order flow cannot even exhibit. The currencies paragraph (§2, lines 117–120) already contains the right defense — the abstract and the "qualitative" sentence just fail to use it. Fix: abstract → "no closed-form constitutive law linking the transverse curvature of a trained potential to lifetime has been measured"; §2 → "these estimator-based (kinematic) lifetime predictions and existence proofs." Cheap, and it removes the paper's single most quotable inconsistency. (I note the task file itself uses "qualitative prediction" — the fix above is *stricter* than the task's boundary and survives a reviewer who has read Mo's full text, which the paper must assume.)

### SHOULD-FIX

**SF-1 — Anchored-run precision/decade mismatches.** §4.4 says ratio "holds to 1.5×10⁻¹²" (✓ backed, `v2-referee-experiments.md:101`: 1.00000 ± ≤1.5e-12 over 4.6 decades); Fig. 3 caption says "1.0000 ± 10⁻¹² … over 4.6 decades" (understates the bound 1.5×); §4.1/G.1 say 4.5 decades for the 150-ep run. All individually correct (150-ep grid spans 4.5 decades, anchored grid 4.6), but nothing tells the reader the grids differ — a false-contradiction trap. One clause each fixes it.

**SF-2 — §4.4 silently merges two experiments' seeds.** "Over 5 seeds, the anchor maintains r\* = 0.911±0.016" is the **anchor-robustness 5-seed run** (`anchor-robustness.md:48`); the surviving-laws numbers (slope −0.956/−0.961, floor, EP 0.5165) are the **3-seed SF-3 re-measurement whose own r\* = 0.917±0.007** (`v2-referee-experiments.md:142`). Fig. 3 says 3 seeds; §4.4 says 5. Both true, unattributed — a C-7 provenance defect a reviewer counting seeds will find.

**SF-3 — No wake–sleep provenance anywhere (C-7).** The §4.4 headline results (8/8 vacuum inversion at 1000 ep — backed, `v2-full-runs.md:24`; the λ=100 anchor cure) depend on sleep_frequency/sleep-steps/CD sampler settings (the source config is "f5/s500/CD", `anchor-robustness.md:146`). None appear in the paper. The 8/8 inversion is irreproducible from the PDF as shipped.

**SF-4 — App D compute table hides the width confound.** CLU is h64; LSTM/LEM cells are h16. **SF-5 — Appendix A contains no prose.** Tex lines 252–258: section header → figure → next section. **SF-6 — Five orphaned references in a hand-built list.** Ramsauer 2021, Karuvally 2025 (EDEN), Jelassi 2024, Hairer 2006, Rusch & Mishra 2021b (UniCORNN) each appear exactly once — as bib entries, cited nowhere (grep-verified against `pj_sub.tex`). (Manual author-year style itself: acceptable at this venue — workshops don't police citation tooling — but it removes the safety net, which is exactly how MF-3 and SF-6 happened.)

**SF-7 — One C-5 scope-free generalization.** §1 lines 47–49: "**Our results hold generally for the class of damped symplectic recurrences**, guided by an exactly-solvable underlying theory." The *laws derive* generally; the *results* are dim-4/S¹/≤5-seed measurements. Reword: "The laws are derived for the class of damped symplectic recurrences; we verify them on one trained instance." (The rest of the draft's C-5 hygiene is genuinely good — the abstract carries dim/seeds/CPU in-sentence.)

**SF-8 — The λ=100 anchor cost is softened below its measured value.** §4.4: "trading off weaker noise rejection and **a higher wake MSE**." The measured cost is **~35× wake MSE** (`anchor-robustness.md:48,64`). Quoting it is a C-6 honesty asset, not a liability — this paper's whole brand is priced tradeoffs.

**SF-9 — "On exact analytic maps, this divergence extends to ≈5×" is weakly wired.** The 5× lives in `mo-deep-read.md:120` (theory-side, pre-trained-measurement) while `v2-full-runs.md:79` attaches "(5× failure)" to the *trained* δ=0.6→4 row whose actual deepest ratio is 0.309 (=3.2×). Either attribute the 5× precisely (analytic prediction, deepest breaking) or drop the sentence — the trained 3.2× is the citable number and carries the claim alone.

Net: the rewrite's defects are *wiring-level* (two slopes, missing qualifiers, orphan refs), not conceptual — a one-day fix pass, not a re-think.
